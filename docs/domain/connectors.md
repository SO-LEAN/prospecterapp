# Connectors

The connections of the members to the services outside Prospecter, Google Calendar first (ADR 0120), through the client each account made at the provider (ADR 0125), the planned exchanges pushed into the calendars of their holders there (ADR 0127), each with where its push stands (ADR 0133), and the import of the e-mails of their mailbox, Gmail, notified through a topic of the account (ADR 0154). Code: `packages/core/src/Connectors`.

## Language

- **Client** (`ProviderClient`): what an account made at a provider for its members to connect through, in its own space there (its Google Cloud): its **credentials** (`ClientCredentials`), the id of the client and its secret, both required (`IncompleteClient`), and, while it has them, its **mailbox notifications** (`MailboxNotifications`): the topic the mailboxes of its members publish their changes to, named in full (`projects/<project>/topics/<topic>`, `InvalidTopic`), and the account of service pushing them to Prospecter. One per account and provider, configured by an administrator of the account.
- **Connection** (`Connection`): the account a member connected at a provider (`externalAccount`), the lasting access it gave (`RefreshToken`), the client of their account it was granted to (its id), and its **state** (`ConnectionState`): **connected**, or **to reconnect** once the provider refused the access. One per member and provider, theirs only.
- **Provider** (`Provider`): the service outside, `google` the one so far.
- **Authorization** (`PendingAuthorization`): what a member asked a provider, waiting for its answer: the `state` the provider gives back, the verifier of its code (PKCE), the scopes asked, bound to the member for ten minutes.
- **Scope** (`Scope`): what Prospecter asks the provider: the calendar, for every connection; the mailbox, for the import of the e-mails (ADR 0154).
- **Grant** (`Grant`): what the provider granted for a code: the external account, its token, and the scopes it granted among those asked, the member unticking one at the consent.
- **Import of the e-mails** (`MailImport`): where it stands on a connection: **off**, **active** while the mailbox of the member notifies Prospecter, **to reconnect** once the provider refused to renew its watch; the point of the history of the mailbox it read up to, when it last read it to its end, until when the mailbox is watched (`MailboxWatch`), the furthest point a notification gave and when.
- **E-mail received** (`ReceivedMail`): a message of the mailbox as the provider gives it: its sender, where the provider files it (its labels), when it was received, its subject (250 characters at most) and its text without the quoted history (10,000 at most), each cut with an ellipsis; one e-mail in every mailbox of the account by its `Message-ID` (ADR 0154). Sent, or in the spam, the trash or the drafts, it is no e-mail received.
- **Correspondent** (`Correspondent`): who of the account an e-mail was exchanged with, by its address: a contact, or else an organization itself.
- **Calendar « Prospecter »**: the calendar Prospecter creates in the account of the member at the provider, and can touch alone (`calendar.app.created`); its id kept with the connection once created.
- **Held exchange** (`HeldExchange`): an exchange as Prospecting tells it now, its holder, where it stands (`ExchangeStatus`), its channel (`Channel`), its moment, its duration, its place or link, whether a video call without a link asks one, its contact and its organization.
- **Event** (`CalendarEvent`): a planned exchange as its holder finds it in their calendar: when, how and where, with whom, the card it leads to, the reminder before it, whether it asks the provider to create its link (`createsALink`, a Google Meet conference); worded by the adapter of the provider.
- **Entry** (`CalendarEntry`): an exchange pushed into the calendar of its holder, kept to withdraw it once Prospecting erased it, and where its push stands.
- **State of the push** (`SyncState`): **pending** until a push reaches the calendar, **synced** once one did, **failed** once the provider refused it for good; since when, and why (`ExchangeSync`).

## Rules

- An administrator of the account configures its client at a provider, or another one (`ConfigureClient`), and removes it (`RemoveClient`, refused without one: `NoClient`); they alone see it, its secret never answered, only its id (`ConsultMyAccountClients`); any other member is refused (`NotTheAdministrator`), whether they are one being read in Accounts (`Members`). The client of an account serves it alone: there is no client of the instance.
- A provider is offered only while a client of the account of the member serves it: without it, it is not offered to the member (`ConsultMyConnections` presents the providers offered, with the id of their client), and an authorization asked of it is refused (`ProviderUnavailable`); the code is exchanged with the client of the account.
- A member asks for an authorization (`AskForAuthorization`), the client drawing its `state` and its verifier; the provider brings them back with a code, which connects their account (`Connect`) while the authorization waits: an answer no authorization of the member awaits, unknown, expired or asked by another member, is refused (`UnknownAuthorization`), and so is a code the provider refuses (`AccessNotGranted`), or a grant without the calendar (`IncompleteGrant`). An access refused at the provider connects nothing; so does a provider that does not answer the exchange of the code: nothing is written, the authorization waits until it expires, and the member goes through the provider again, its code serving once.
- Connecting again replaces the account and the token of the connection, connected again; the former access to another account is revoked at the provider, that of the same account is not, Google revoking with a token the whole grant of its account (`Connection::renew()`).
- A connection is checked with its provider through the client of the account (`CheckMyConnections`, as the page of the connectors opens): a token the provider refuses, or a token granted to another client than that of the account (the administrator changed it), makes it **to reconnect** (`Connection::check()`), once; a new secret of the same client keeps it; without a client of the account it is not checked, kept as last seen; a provider that does not answer leaves it as last seen too, the check failing.
- Disconnecting revokes the access at the provider, then forgets the connection (`Disconnect`), refused without a connection (`NoConnection`); a revocation failing does not keep it.
- Each change of an exchange planned, cancelled or erased is pushed (`PushExchange`, reacting to `ExchangeFactsChanged`, the bus passing it those facts only), the exchange read again from Prospecting, the latest state winning whatever the order of the deliveries (ADR 0127): planned, a call, a meeting or a video call is put into the calendar « Prospecter » of its holder, created at the first push and again once its member deleted it, its contact named as its holder reads them (`ContactNames`), reminded at the delay of the holder, none when they turned their reminders off; done or missed, its event stays as it was, but put again without the name of its contact once forgotten, leading to the organization (`UnnameForgottenContact`, reacting to `ContactForgotten`, the entries found by their contact); cancelled, or erased (its contact forgotten cancels it while still to come, ADR 0112), it is withdrawn, an erased one from the calendar of its entry, kept only for a push that reached the calendar. Without a connection of the holder, connected, nor a client of their account, nothing is asked of the provider; a token of another client, or one the provider refuses, makes the connection **to reconnect**, nothing tried again; a push the provider refuses for good (`CalendarRefusedThePush`) is kept on its entry, failed, the reaction succeeding (ADR 0133), a withdrawal refused alike when it has an entry; a push reaching the calendar makes its entry synced, one reaching nothing, the connection to reconnect, makes an entry already there pending. A push writes the connection it read only while it is still the one kept, connected at the same moment (`Connection::isTheSameAs()`): one its member disconnected, or connected again, meanwhile is not written over, the push tried again through the new one, or none (ADR 0133, 0140).
- A planned video call asking its link, and having none, is put with the request of a conference, identified by its exchange so that the provider creates it once however many times it is pushed; the link the provider answers is published (`VideoCallLinkCreated`, recorded by `Connection::push()`), for Prospecting to keep on the exchange, which is pushed again with it (ADR 0137). Asking nothing, an event keeps the conference the provider has.
- Where the push of an exchange stands is read by its holder only, while they have a connection (`ConsultExchangeSync`): as its entry keeps it, pending while an exchange they planned and still to come was never pushed, with the reason `toReconnect` while their connection is to reconnect; nothing for the exchange of another member, nor for one past never pushed. Their connections count their exchanges to come synced and failed (`ConsultMyConnections`).
- The holder pushes their exchange again (`RetryPush`, refused to anyone else: `NotTheHolder`), or every one of theirs failed (`RetryFailedPushes`), read again from Prospecting as the reaction does.
- Once the member connected, for the first time or again (`Connected`), or once their calendar was created again (`CalendarRecreated`), the exchanges they still have planned from that moment on are pushed (`PushUpcomingExchanges`), the 40 soonest, read from Prospecting (`ConsultPlannedExchangesService`).
- An administrator of the account names the topic of its client and the account of service pushing its notifications (`ConfigureMailboxNotifications`), or removes them (`RemoveMailboxNotifications`), refused without a client (`NoClient`) and to any other member (`NotTheAdministrator`); the account of service of an account is told without a member, to trust a notification (`ConsultMailboxPusher`, ADR 0154).
- The import of the e-mails is offered while the client of the account has a topic (`ConsultMyConnections` presents it with the providers offered), and, by the API, to a member allowed to manage the personal data (ADR 0081): a member asks the reading of their mailbox with their calendar (`AskToImportMail`, refused without a topic: `MailImportUnavailable`), the provider asked for the account connected (the API names it to Google, `login_hint`), then bringing them back as for a connection (`Connect`): granted by another account, that account is connected instead, as by connecting again, and its mailbox imported; granted, their mailbox is watched through the topic, all but the spam, the trash and the drafts, and the import is active from where its history stands at that moment (`Connection::importMail()`, `MailImportStarted`); the mailbox unticked at the consent, the import stays off and the calendar connected.
- The member stops the import (`StopMailImport`): the watch stopped at the provider through the client of the account, a failure ignored, the watch expiring within its week (`MailImportStopped`), the exchanges already imported staying, the calendar connected; disconnecting stops it too (`Disconnect`), and connecting another account forgets the import of the former mailbox.
- Each day, the watch of every import active is renewed (`RenewMailWatch`, `MailWatchRenewed`), through the topic the account has now, nothing renewed while it has none; a token the provider refuses, or one that no longer grants the mailbox, makes the import **to reconnect** (`MailImportLost`), the member activating it again.
- A change notified by a mailbox (`NoteMailboxNotification`, without a member: the provider pushed it) is noted on the connection of the member of the account importing it: the furthest point of its history notified, whatever the order of the notifications, and when (`MailboxChanged`); a mailbox no member of the account imports, or an import not active, notes nothing.
- Once a change was noted, or the watch renewed, the history of the mailbox is read from the point read last (`ReadMailboxHistory`, `Connection::readMailboxHistory()`): 500 messages at most, each to import on its own (`MailboxMessageAdded`), the rest at the next reading; a history the provider no longer keeps is read again by the date of its last reading to its end, the 1,000 newest e-mails received since, the older ones lost; a token refused makes the import **to reconnect** (`MailImportLost`); a member no longer managing the personal data has their import stopped (ADR 0154). A notification lost is caught up by the next one, or by the daily renewal.
- Each message read is imported (`ImportMail`) when it is an e-mail received from a contact of the account, or else from the address of an organization of the account (`Correspondents`, asked of PersonalData and Prospecting): the exchange is told to Prospecting with its subject and its text (`EmailExchanged`, published by the reaction itself, no domain event carrying the text); an e-mail sent, in the spam, the trash or the drafts, from an unknown sender, or a person in copy, is nothing to import, and no contact is ever created. A message gone meanwhile, or an import no longer active, imports nothing.
- A member reads their own connections only (`ConsultMyConnections`); the address of their external account, personal data kept outside PersonalData, is theirs alone and forgotten when they disconnect (ADR 0120).

## What it publishes

The service `WhichHaveAConnectedCalendarService`: which of the members asked have their connection to Google connected, one to reconnect not (`WhichHaveAConnectedCalendar`, ADR 0148), for Booking. The integration event `VideoCallLinkCreated` (the account, the holder, the exchange, the link the provider created, ADR 0137), translated from its domain event of the same name (`PublishVideoCallLinkCreated`), and `EmailExchanged` (the account, the member, the provider, the identity of the e-mail, the direction, the contact or the organization, the subject, the text, when it was exchanged, ADR 0154), published by `ImportMail`; its other domain events (`Connected`, `ConnectionLost`, `Disconnected`, `ClientConfigured`, `ClientRemoved`, `CalendarRecreated`, `MailImportStarted`, `MailImportStopped`, `MailImportLost`, `MailWatchRenewed`, `MailboxChanged`, `MailboxMessageAdded`) stay in the context. It reads the account of a member, whether they administer it, their reminder and whether they manage the personal data, from Accounts (`Members`, `ConsultMembersService`), who an address designates from PersonalData and Prospecting (`Correspondents`, `WhichContactsHaveTheseEmailsService`, `WhichOrganizationsHaveTheseEmailsService`), the exchanges from Prospecting (`HeldExchanges`, `ConsultHeldExchangesService`, `ConsultPlannedExchangesService`), the names of the contacts from PersonalData (`ContactNames`, `NameContactsService`), and reacts to the facts of the exchanges (`ExchangeFactsChanged`) and to the contacts forgotten (`ContactForgotten`).
