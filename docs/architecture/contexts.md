# The bounded contexts

What is in force; the reasons are in the records it names (`docs/adr`), read only to know why or before changing it (ADR 0090).

Accounts, PersonalData and Prospecting (ADR 0030, 0031), Reporting downstream of them (ADR 0103), Connectors, the connections of the members to the services outside (ADR 0120), Campaigns, the tags of the organizations and the contacts, downstream of them (ADR 0138), Booking, the booking page of each member, read without signing in (ADR 0148), and the shared kernel of what they all share (`core.md`). Their language and their rules: `docs/domain/accounts.md`, `personal-data.md`, `prospecting.md`, `reporting.md`, `connectors.md`, `campaigns.md`, `booking.md`.

## What a context sees of another

- A context never uses another one: it declares a port in its own words (`Prospectors`, `Readers`, `KnownContacts`, `ProspectedOrganizations`), implemented outside the core by an anticorruption adapter (`App\Prospecting\AntiCorruption\AccountsProspectors`, `App\PersonalData\AntiCorruption\AccountsReaders`, `App\Prospecting\AntiCorruption\PersonalDataKnownContacts`, `App\PersonalData\AntiCorruption\ProspectingOrganizations`, `App\Connectors\AntiCorruption\AccountsMembers`: the account of a member who connects, whether they administer it, their reminder and whether they manage the personal data; `ProspectingHeldExchanges`, `PersonalDataContactNames`: the exchange Connectors pushes, the name of its contact; `PersonalDataAndProspectingCorrespondents`: the contact, or the organization, an address of an e-mail designates, ADR 0154; `App\Campaigns\AntiCorruption\AccountsTaggers`, `ProspectingTaggableOrganizations`, `PersonalDataTaggableContacts`: the account of a member who tags, whether the account prospects an organization or knows a contact; `AccountsCampaigners`, `PersonalDataAndProspectingAddressees`: the account of a member who runs campaigns, what a message names a contact by, ADR 0144; `App\Prospecting\AntiCorruption\CampaignsCampaignRecipients`, `CampaignsCampaignNames`: whether a contact is a recipient still to handle of a started campaign, the names of the campaigns of a timeline, ADR 0145; `App\Booking\AntiCorruption\AccountsAndPersonalDataHosts`, `ConnectorsConnectedCalendars`, `ProspectingHostAgendas`: the account of a host, its corporate name and how the host presents themselves, whether their calendar is connected, the exchanges they still have planned, ADR 0148, 0153), doubled in the scenarios by the same code (`tests/Double`). The adapter sees of the other context only what it publishes (ADR 0045): the service its `Contract/Service` declares (ADR 0089: `ConsultMembersService`, `WhichAreContactsService`, `WhichAreProspectedService`, `ConsultHeldExchangesService`, `NameContactsService`, `PresentMembersService`, `NameOrganizationsOfContactsService`, `WhichRecipientsArePendingService`, `NameCampaignsService`, `WhichHaveAConnectedCalendarService`, `WhichContactsHaveTheseEmailsService`, `WhichOrganizationsHaveTheseEmailsService`), injected by its type, implemented by the handler of the query in its `Application`, its answer kept by `KeptMembers`, `KeptContacts`, `KeptProspected`. A published query asks about a set, a single element being a set of one, and refuses nothing: what is unknown is absent from its answer, the client deciding what an absence means; it answers plain data with only what the others need, checking no caller, fixed by `features/langage-publie.feature`; `tests/Architecture/BoundedContextsTest` fails otherwise. A port of the asking context asks about a set where a use case asks about several elements (`ProspectedOrganizations::prospectedAmong()`). A new question between contexts is a new published service, its scenario, and the rule it answers in the aggregate (`Person::isAContactOf()`).

## What each publishes

| Context | Services (`Contract/Service`, ADR 0089) | Integration events (`Contract/Event`, ADR 0046) |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts | `ConsultMembersService`: who the members are, their reminder, the corporate name of their account; `ListAccountsService`: the identities of the accounts, by slice | `AccountOpened`, `MemberRegistered` |
| PersonalData | `WhichAreContactsService`: which persons are contacts of an account; `WhichContactsHaveTheseEmailsService`: which contacts of an account have addresses (ADR 0154); `NameContactsService`: the names, the civilities and the job titles of persons as a member reads them; `PresentMembersService`: the name and the picture of members, to whom they give a link; `ListContactFactsService`: the facts of the contacts of an account, by slice | `ContactCollected`, `ContactForgotten`, `ContactFactsChanged` |
| Prospecting | `WhichAreProspectedService`: which organizations an account prospects; `WhichOrganizationsHaveTheseEmailsService`: which organizations of an account have addresses (ADR 0154); `NameOrganizationsOfContactsService`: the names of the organizations of contacts of an account, the first first; `ConsultHeldExchangesService`: exchanges as their holders hold them, and `ConsultPlannedExchangesService`: those a holder still has planned from a moment on; `ListOrganizationFactsService`, `ListExchangeFactsService`: the facts of the organizations and of the exchanges of an account, by slice | `ContactsLeftWithoutOrganization`, `ContactLeftUnattached` (ADR 0146), `OrganizationFactsChanged`, `ExchangeFactsChanged` |
| Reporting | nothing | nothing |
| Connectors | `WhichHaveAConnectedCalendarService`: which members have their calendar connected, a connection to reconnect not being one (ADR 0148) | `VideoCallLinkCreated` (ADR 0137), `EmailExchanged` (ADR 0154) |
| Campaigns | `WhichRecipientsArePendingService`: whether a campaign is started, its channel, which contacts it keeps and has still to handle; `NameCampaignsService`: the names of campaigns of an account (ADR 0145) | nothing |
| Booking | nothing | nothing |

A context counting what another one holds reads its **facts** (ADR 0099): a snapshot of an aggregate published each time it changes (`OrganizationFactsChanged`: present, located, the contacts it names, the code of its municipality and the country of its address, ADR 0142; `ExchangeFactsChanged`: present, the status and the moment of the exchange, its contact and its campaign, ADR 0145; `ContactFactsChanged`: present, how it is reached: e-mail, phone, mobile, LinkedIn, booleans only, ADR 0141), by a translator receiving every domain event of the aggregate (`PublishOrganizationFactsHandler` of `OrganizationEvent`, `PublishExchangeFactsHandler` of `ExchangeEvent`, `PublishContactFactsHandler` of `PersonEvent`, which publishes nothing for the profile of a member, no contact) before the commit, with the revision its repository writes (`persistence.md`), the highest the latest; an aggregate removed gives a terminal fact, absent at the revision 0. The service of the same facts gives them again by slice, to count everything from the state; both read one retriever (`OrganizationFactsRetriever`, `ExchangeFactsRetriever`, `ContactFactsRetriever`). `ListAccountsService` tells which accounts there are to count (`AccountListRetriever`). Reporting counts them (ADR 0103): its reactions record the facts received (`RecordOrganizationFacts`, `RecordExchangeFacts`, `RecordContactFacts`), and a rebuild reads them again through its ports `SourceAccounts`, `ProspectingFacts` and `PersonalDataFacts`, the anticorruption layers asking the services by slice (`App\Reporting\AntiCorruption\AccountsSourceAccounts`, `ProspectingFactsFromProspecting`, `PersonalDataFactsFromPersonalData`); in the API, the subscription `reporting-figures` delivers it the facts, and `app:reporting:rebuild` counts again (ADR 0104, `persistence.md`). Connectors takes a fact of an exchange as the sign that it changed, and reads it again from `ConsultHeldExchangesService` to push it into the calendar of its holder, the latest state winning whatever the order of the deliveries (ADR 0127, `docs/domain/connectors.md`); once its member connected, or their calendar was created again, it reads their exchanges to come from `ConsultPlannedExchangesService` to push them (ADR 0133). A video call asking its link (`linkRequested` of `ConsultHeldExchangesService`) is pushed with the request of a conference; the link the provider created is published (`VideoCallLinkCreated`, translated by `PublishVideoCallLinkCreatedHandler` from the domain event of the connection), and Prospecting keeps it on the exchange (`KeepCreatedVideoCallLink`, the subscription `prospecting-keep-created-video-call-link`), which is pushed again, its link now in its facts (ADR 0137). Campaigns forgets the tags of an organization at its terminal fact, and those of a contact once forgotten (`ContactForgotten`, ADR 0138); it keeps the facts of the organizations and of the contacts (`RecordOrganizationFacts`, `RecordContactFacts`, the subscription `campaigns-record-facts`, from their second version) and derives from them the tags a contact inherits, the geographic tags of an organization (ADR 0142) and those of how a contact is reached, its rebuild reading them again through its ports `TaggedAccounts`, `OrganizationsAndTheirContacts` and `ContactsReach` (`App\Campaigns\AntiCorruption\AccountsTaggedAccounts`, `ProspectingOrganizationsAndTheirContacts`, `PersonalDataContactsReach`, ADR 0141, `docs/domain/campaigns.md`). Prospecting asks Campaigns, before noting the exchange of a recipient of a campaign, whether the contact is a recipient still to handle of a started campaign (`WhichRecipientsArePendingService`), and Campaigns processes the recipient from the facts of the exchange, which name its campaign and its contact (`ProcessRecipientOfCampaignExchange`, the subscription `campaigns-process-recipient`); a timeline and the detail of an exchange name its campaign by `NameCampaignsService` (ADR 0145). The terminal fact of an exchange erased still names its contact and its campaign, which its erasure tells, and whether it went with its organization; Campaigns brings back to handle the recipient it processed when a member erased it (`ReopenRecipientOfErasedExchange`, the subscription `campaigns-reopen-recipient`, ADR 0152, 0155). Connectors recognizes the sender of an e-mail received by asking PersonalData which contact has its address, and Prospecting which organization (`WhichContactsHaveTheseEmailsService`, `WhichOrganizationsHaveTheseEmailsService`), and tells Prospecting the e-mail exchanged, its subject and its text (`EmailExchanged`, published by `ImportMailHandler`), which Prospecting notes as an exchange of the member (`NoteImportedEmail`, the subscription `prospecting-note-imported-email`, ADR 0154).

In the API, the anticorruption layers implementing the ports of a context are in `src/<Context>/AntiCorruption` (ADR 0086), the delivery of a context uses nothing of another but its Contract (`api.md`).

## How events travel

The whole mechanism, drawn, with its failures and its operations: `messaging.md`.

Every event leaves its context in the shape of an event of EventBridge (ADR 0097). With the delivery `outbox` (`INTEGRATION_EVENTS_DELIVERY`, the local stack and the instances, ADR 0108), it is written with the command and relayed to the bus of EventBridge (ADR 0098, `persistence.md`), whose rules give it to the worker, which runs the reactions after the command, each in its own transaction (ADR 0101, [below](#the-worker)). The tests keep the delivery `sync`, and so does an instance naming it in its value `integration_events_delivery` (`infra.md`): every event delivered at once, within the command, a reaction refusing refusing the gesture.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `source` | `prospecter.<context>` (`prospecter.accounts`, `prospecter.personal-data`, `prospecter.prospecting`, `prospecter.connectors`) for an integration event; `prospecter.<context>.internal` for a domain event |
| `detail-type` | the short name of the class followed by the version of its contract: `ContactCollected.v1` |
| `detail` | `{"eventId": "<uuid v7>", "occurredAt": "<ISO 8601 UTC, microseconds>", "correlationId": "<uuid v7>", "causationId": "<uuid v7 or null>", "depth": 0, "payload": {…}}` |

- The classes are listed explicitly, by source and detail-type, in `App\Shared\Messaging\IntegrationEventTypes` and `DomainEventTypes`; a domain event and an integration event of the same name (`AccountOpened`) are told apart by their source. `EventCodec` writes the payload by the parameters of the constructor: an identity as its string, a moment as the documents write it (`Shared\Persistence\Documents::moment()`), a list element by element, read back by the types of the parameters and their docblocks. The PHP contract carries no identity: the `eventId` belongs to the envelope. A new event class goes in its list, or `tests/Shared/Messaging/EventCodecTest` fails; each survives a round trip through JSON. A change of the payload of an event bumps its version once an outbox or a queue of failures may hold the former shape, as those of the instances may since they deliver `outbox` (ADR 0108): the class is written in its current version (`TYPES`) and still read in the former one (`FORMER_TYPES`), a parameter added since read as its constructor default, null when it has none (ADR 0155), the reaction listening to both (`ExchangeFactsChanged.v1`, without the status, read as held by Reporting).
- No loop: an event published by a command has the depth 0, no cause, and the correlation of the command (`Shared\Messaging\CorrelatedCommands`, a middleware of `command.bus`); an event published by a reaction to an event of depth n has the depth n + 1, the same correlation, and the `eventId` it reacted to as its cause; the integration event a translator publishes keeps the depth of its domain event. Beyond `EventLoop::MAX_EVENT_DEPTH` (5), the synchronous delivery throws `EventLoop`, and the worker refuses the event.

## The subscriptions

The reactions are listed in one place (ADR 0097), `apps/api/config/subscriptions.json`, read by Symfony (the parameter `app.subscriptions`) and later by the infrastructure. One entry per subscription, named `<context>-<intent>`, of the context of its reaction:

```json
"prospecting-stop-keeping-exchanges": {
  "context": "prospecting",
  "sources": ["prospecter.prospecting.internal"],
  "events": ["ProspectingStopped.v1"],
  "handler": "Prospecter\\Core\\Prospecting\\Application\\StopKeepingExchanges\\StopKeepingExchangesHandler",
  "inbox": true
}
```

A subscription names its reaction (`handler`), or one per event it listens to (`handlers`, by detail-type, ADR 0104): `reporting-figures` gives each fact to the reaction of its kind:

```json
"reporting-figures": {
  "context": "reporting",
  "sources": ["prospecter.prospecting", "prospecter.personal-data"],
  "events": ["OrganizationFactsChanged.v1", "OrganizationFactsChanged.v2", "OrganizationFactsChanged.v3", "ExchangeFactsChanged.v1", "ExchangeFactsChanged.v2", "ContactFactsChanged.v1", "ContactFactsChanged.v2"],
  "handlers": {"OrganizationFactsChanged.v1": "…RecordOrganizationFactsHandler", "OrganizationFactsChanged.v2": "…RecordOrganizationFactsHandler", "OrganizationFactsChanged.v3": "…RecordOrganizationFactsHandler", "ExchangeFactsChanged.v1": "…RecordExchangeFactsHandler", "ExchangeFactsChanged.v2": "…RecordExchangeFactsHandler", "ContactFactsChanged.v1": "…RecordContactFactsHandler", "ContactFactsChanged.v2": "…RecordContactFactsHandler"},
  "inbox": false
}
```

A subscription listening to some payloads only names them (`payload`, a pattern of EventBridge on the payload of the detail, `$or` and the values a field may hold, ADR 0127): its rule of the bus passes those only, and the delivery `sync` reads it alike (`Shared\Messaging\PayloadPattern`, `tests/Shared/Messaging/SubscriptionsTest`); `connectors-push-exchange` listens to the facts of the exchanges planned, cancelled or erased, the bulk of the facts asking the worker nothing; `campaigns-forget-tags-of-unprospected-organization` to the terminal facts of the organizations (`{"present": [false]}`).

Two handlers of one event make two subscriptions. `inbox` (ADR 0102): the worker applies an event once per subscription, for a reaction whose second application would change something; a reaction removing a file goes without, and so do those of Campaigns forgetting a tagging or recording a fact, and Reporting, the revision of a fact making its second application harmless. A service of the tests may be a subscription declared by its tag `app.subscription` (`Tests\App\Double\FailingReaction`), unknown to the infrastructure. A translator (`Application/Publish…/Publish…Handler`) is no subscription: it stays on the event bus, publishing a domain event for the other contexts. `tests/Architecture/ReactionsAreSubscribedTest` fails when an event handler of the core is neither a translator nor a subscription, when a subscription names an unknown reaction, neither or both of `handler` and `handlers`, handlers for other events than its own, or an event none of its sources publishes, or when a subscription to a source `.internal` is of another context than the event: a domain event never leaves its context (ADR 0046). `StopKeepingExchanges` publishes an `ExchangeLeftWithoutOrganization` per exchange to erase, and `prospecting-erase-exchange` erases each one, so that each erasure fits in its own transaction (`persistence.md`); delivered again for an exchange erased already, it writes nothing. A file removed leaves the storage in a reaction of Prospecting to the event naming it (`prospecting-discard-removed-logo`, `prospecting-discard-removed-attachment`, `prospecting-discard-attachments-of-erased-exchange`), once its removal is written. The order of the reactions is no promise.

## The worker

The function `prospecter-<instance>-api-worker` (`App\Shared\Messaging\IntegrationEventConsumer`, ADR 0101), the package and the environment of the API, its delivery `outbox`, invoked asynchronously by a rule of the bus per subscription (named after it, `infra.md`) with `{"subscription": "<name>", "source": …, "detailType": …, "detail": {…}}`.

- An invocation reads the event (`Invocation`, `EventCodec`), refuses one deeper than 5, opens a unit of work, keeps the inbox of the subscription when it has one (`Inbox`: PK `INBOX#<subscription>`, SK the `eventId`, `expiresAt` 15 days later, in the table of its context, under `attribute_not_exists(SK)`), calls the reaction through its decorators within the cause of the event (what it publishes one level deeper, `Causation`), and commits: the inbox, what the reaction writes, the outbox of what it publishes. An event applied already is found at the commit (`AlreadyApplied`): the invocation ends normally, nothing written. A transaction cancelled by a condition carrying no refusal, or by a conflict with another transaction (`ConditionFailed`), is applied again from its reads, three times at most after a random wait under 100 ms, then thrown (ADR 0104).
- A failure is logged with the `eventId`, the correlation and, for a refusal, its `reason` and `field`, then sorted. Deterministic: a `UseCaseRefused`, a `BusinessException` detected at the commit, an `UnreadableEvent` (an unknown detail-type or version, a detail that does not fit), an `UnknownSubscription` (or an event the subscription does not listen to), an `EventLoop`: the worker puts the invocation in the queue `integration-failures` (`IntegrationFailures`, in the envelope of the failures of Lambda, `requestContext.condition` `Refused`) and ends normally. Any other is thrown: Lambda retries twice, then puts it in the same queue. A reaction with nothing left to do (its object gone) ends without throwing; one missing an earlier fact throws, to be retried.
- `bin/console app:integration:redrive` invokes the worker again, asynchronously, with the `requestPayload` of each message of the queue, then deletes it; a message keeping none is left there, the command failing. An event refused is redriven once what refused it is corrected and deployed.
