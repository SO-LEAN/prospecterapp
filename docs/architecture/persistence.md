# Persistence

What is in force; the reasons are in the records it names (`docs/adr`), read only to know why or before changing it (ADR 0090).

The aggregates live in DynamoDB, one table per bounded context (ADR 0038, 0048); the queries read their read models in the same tables, without restoring the aggregates (ADR 0088).

## Tables and items

Each bounded context has its own DynamoDB table (ADR 0038, 0048): `<DYNAMODB_TABLE_PREFIX>-<context>` (`prospecter-dev-prospecting`; the prefix `prospecter-test` in tests), a `Shared\Persistence\Table` injected by name in the persistence of its context only (`Table $prospectingTable`); `tests/Architecture/TablePerContextTest` fails otherwise. Nothing spans two contexts, but the check of the consistency, which reads them all and writes none (`Consistency\ContextTables`, `api.md`, ADR 0105). Locally the tables live in MiniStack (`ministack` service of `docker-compose.yml`, `DYNAMODB_ENDPOINT`, dev credentials in `.env`); `Shared\Persistence\Tables` creates them all alike (`PK`/`SK`, the indexes `GSI1` and `GSI2`, and `NAME`, `EMAIL` and `PHONE` finding an aggregate, billed by request, their new items given to a stream (`NEW_IMAGE`) and an item forgotten past its `expiresAt`, an epoch (TTL); on AWS `infra/tables.tf`, alike), `make db` with the data of the demonstration (`Demo\DemoData`, written through the domain and publishing its events like a use case).

- Single-table design, each repository documenting its items: `ORGANIZATION#<id>` listed by account in `GSI1` and by holding in `GSI2`, the links `CONTACT#<person>` / `ORGANIZATION#<id>`; `EXCHANGE#<id>` in the timeline of its contact in `GSI1` (`TIMELINE#<account>#<person>`, by its moment then its id, ADR 0074) and among those of its account in `GSI2` (`ACCOUNT_EXCHANGES#<account>`, by its id, ADR 0099), the links `EXCHANGES#<organization>` / `EXCHANGE#<id>`, in the timeline of the organization in `GSI1` (`ORGANIZATION_TIMELINE#<organization>`, ADR 0076) and, while their exchange is to make conform, in `GSI2` (`TO_MAKE_CONFORM#<organization>`, counted there, ADR 0079); while an exchange is planned, its link `AGENDA#<member>` / `EXCHANGE#<id>` in the agenda of its holder, in `GSI1` under its position (ADR 0111); a timeline read below the position of the moment of the reading for the past (`Table::query()`, the greatest first), above it for what is to come (`Table::queryAfter()`, the smallest first, `SortKeyRange`); `PERSON#<id>` listed by account and purpose; `ACCOUNT#<id>` listed with every account in `GSI1` (`ACCOUNTS`, by its id, ADR 0099), `MEMBER#<id>`; the versions, facts and figures of Reporting ([below](#reporting)); `OUTBOX#<commit>` / `OUTBOX`, the outbox of a commit ([below](#the-outbox)); `INBOX#<subscription>` / `<eventId>`, an event a subscription applied ([below](#the-inbox)).
- An aggregate is stored as a plain document (`OrganizationDocument`, `PersonDocument`, `Shared\Persistence\Documents` for the value objects of the shared kernel, moments in UTC to the microsecond) and restored without its constructor by `Shared\Persistence\Hydrator`: the domain keeps its value objects and private properties (ADR 0028, 0048); a new property of an aggregate or a value object needs a change of its document.
- The item of an organization, of an exchange and of a person carries its `revision`, incremented by `save()` (`Shared\Persistence\Revision`): the revision of its facts (ADR 0099), unknown to the aggregate, no optimistic lock; an item written before it reads as the revision 0 until its next save.
- `save()` writes the aggregate and what follows from it, in the unit of work of the command: the reservation of its email (`EMAIL#...`, written under a condition and carrying the refusal `EmailAlreadyUsed`, thrown when a second holder cancels the transaction: the safety net of `EmailUniqueness`, ADR 0039, 0048), the links of its contacts.
- Lists come in slices newest first after a cursor (ADR 0049); a search finds an aggregate by the beginning of its name or of its email (ADR 0071): its item carries its search keys as the keys of the indexes `NAME` and `EMAIL`, and its phone number in E.164 as the key of `PHONE` (ADR 0072) (`Shared\Persistence\SearchIndexes`, keys only), written with it; `bin/console app:keys:rebuild` (`Keys\RebuildKeysCommand`, composing the contexts) writes again the keys the tables derive from the aggregates, as many times as needed: the item of every organization and of every person, its search keys with it, removing the items of the search before ADR 0071 (`TOKEN#...`), and the item of every exchange, its key by account with it, and of every account, its key in the list of the accounts with it (ADR 0099), saying how many of each it wrote.

## The unit of work

What a command writes is kept all at once or not at all (ADR 0095), and so is what a reaction writes, in a transaction of its own after the command (ADR 0101), its inbox with it; with the delivery `sync` (the tests, an instance naming it), the reactions still join the unit of the command. `Shared\Persistence\DynamoUnitOfWork` implements the port `UnitOfWork` of the core, opened by the decorators `Transactional…Handler` of each command and reaction (`api.md`), and by the worker around a reaction (`contexts.md`); a gesture runs one use case (ADR 0100).

- While a unit is open, `Table::transact()` keeps its writes (`Write`: a put or a delete of an item, made by `putting()` and `deleting()`, a put whose condition is about the item as stored by `replacing()`, an addition to numbers of an item by `adding()`), one per item (table, PK, SK), the last one winning, except two additions, kept as one, their sum, and a `replacing()` following a put of its item, which keeps the condition of the first (ADR 0104); the outermost commit sends them in one `TransactWriteItems`, across the tables. Without an open unit (`Demo\DemoData`, the commands of the console that rebuild), a write is sent at once; `Table::batch()` is always sent at once, for a projection that can be rebuilt.
- More than 100 writes: the commit throws a `LogicException` before sending anything; a transaction is never split. Each exchange of an organization no longer prospected is erased by its own reaction (`contexts.md`), so that stopping the prospecting of an organization of many exchanges fits; with the delivery `sync`, its reactions joining the command, it is refused beyond some thirty.
- A conditional write carries what to throw when its condition does not hold (`EmailAlreadyUsed::byAnother(...)`, `AlreadyApplied` for an inbox, given the cancellation as its cause); the `CancellationReasons` name the write, the first one carrying something winning; the decorators of the handlers translate a refusal like any (409). A condition carrying none throws `ConditionFailed`.
- A read by key (`item()`, `items()`) and the query of a partition (`query()`, of the table or of an index) see what the open unit is about to write: an item put reads as written, an item deleted as absent, an item added to as stored. `count()`, `keysAt()`, `keysBeginningWith()` and `scan()` read the table as it was before the commit: no command reads them.
- A `discard()` without its `commit()` abandons the whole unit until its outermost level ends: Messenger still calls the other handlers of an event after one threw, and what they write is thrown away too.
- A file sent to the storage by a use case whose unit is discarded afterwards stays there, unused: the storage is outside the transaction. A file is removed from it by a reaction once its removal is written (ADR 0101, `contexts.md`).

## The outbox

With the delivery `outbox` (`INTEGRATION_EVENTS_DELIVERY=outbox`, `api.md`), what a command or a reaction publishes is written with what it changes, all or nothing, and published on the bus after the commit (ADR 0098), whose rules give it to the worker (`contexts.md`); the local stack and the instances deliver so (ADR 0108), the tests stay `sync`.

- `Shared\Messaging\OutboxDelivery` keeps each event in `Shared\Messaging\CommitOutbox`, the state of the request (`ResetInterface`), an event published again by the commit (the same source, type and payload: the facts of an aggregate, published for each of its domain events) once (ADR 0104); the outermost commit of `DynamoUnitOfWork` adds to its `TransactWriteItems` one item, in the table of the context the events happened in, named by their source, and a discarded unit throws its events away. A transaction spans one use case, hence one context: a commit publishing in two contexts throws a `LogicException` before anything is sent: no gesture does, each running one use case (ADR 0100). No event, no item. An event published outside a unit (`Demo\DemoData`) is a commit of its own.
- The item (`Shared\Persistence\OutboxItem`): PK `OUTBOX#<commit>` (a UUID v7), SK `OUTBOX`, under `attribute_not_exists(PK)`; `events` the list of the events as they travel (`{"source", "detailType", "detail"}`, `contexts.md`), a JSON string; `occurredAt` the moment of the commit (the real clock, `app.commit_clock`); `expiresAt` an epoch a week later, when the table forgets it. Its events over 400 KB: the commit throws a `LogicException` before sending anything. It counts in the 100 writes of a transaction.
- The stream of the table gives the new item to the relay (`Shared\Messaging\OutboxRelay`, `infra.md`, `local-stack.md`), which publishes its events on the bus by `Shared\Messaging\IntegrationEventBus` (`INTEGRATION_EVENT_BUS`, `EVENTBRIDGE_ENDPOINT` locally): ten entries per `PutEvents`, an entry refused failing the whole batch (`EventsNotPublished`), given again by the stream. It writes nothing; it logs each event published with its `eventId` and its correlation.
- `bin/console app:outbox:republish --since=<moment>` publishes again the items the tables keep, those of the commits since the moment (`Shared\Persistence\OutboxItems`): an event may be published twice, the reactions are to be idempotent.

## The inbox

A subscription whose reaction would change something applied twice (`"inbox": true`, `config/subscriptions.json`) applies an event once (ADR 0102): `Shared\Messaging\Inbox` puts, in the unit of work of the reaction, an item in the table of the context of the subscription, PK `INBOX#<subscription>`, SK the `eventId`, `receivedAt` the moment (`app.commit_clock`), `expiresAt` an epoch 15 days later, under `attribute_not_exists(SK)`; written first, its condition failing wins over the others and throws `AlreadyApplied`. It counts in the 100 writes of a transaction; a reaction that fails writes none. Two subscriptions to one event each have theirs.

## Reporting

The table of Reporting (`<prefix>-reporting`, ADR 0103, 0104) keeps figures derived from the other contexts, recalculable: no personal data, identities and booleans.

| Item | PK | SK | Attributes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active version | `PROJECTION` | `ACTIVE` | `version` |
| Version being built | `PROJECTION` | `BUILDING` | `version`, `since` |
| Fact of an organization | `FACTS#<version>#<account>` | `ORGANIZATION#<id>` | `revision`, `present`, `located` |
| Fact of an exchange | `FACTS#<version>#<account>` | `EXCHANGE#<id>` | `revision`, `present`, `month` (YYYY-MM, null without a moment) |
| Fact of a contact | `FACTS#<version>#<account>` | `CONTACT#<id>` | `revision`, `present` |
| Figures of an account | `FIGURES#<version>#<account>` | `TOTAL` | `activeOrganizations`, `locatedOrganizations`, `contacts`, `exchanges` |
| Figures of a month | `FIGURES#<version>#<account>` | `MONTH#<YYYY-MM>` | `exchanges` |

- `DynamoFactRepository::record()` writes a fact in one transaction: the fact by `replacing()` under `attribute_not_exists(SK)`, or `revision = :revision AND present = :present` of the fact it replaces, read before by a consistent read; an `ADD` of the non-zero differences on `TOTAL`, and on the former and the new `MONTH#…` when the month changes. A terminal fact gets an `expiresAt` 15 days later, longer than an event may wait (6 hours in the worker, a day of retries of EventBridge, 14 days in the failures). A condition failing, or a `TransactionConflict`, cancels it (`ConditionFailed`): the worker applies the reaction again from its reads ([contexts.md](contexts.md#the-worker)). The live versions of a rebuild are written in the transaction of the reaction.
- `DynamoProjectionVersions`: `abandonBuilding()` removes `BUILDING` in its unit; `removeAbandoned()` removes every version but the active one (what an interrupted rebuild left, what a recording wrote in the former version after its removal: `scan()` of `FACTS#` and `FIGURES#`, `batch()`), once the recordings that saw the version abandoned live ended (ADR 0106); `startBuilding()` writes `BUILDING` in its unit; `activate()` writes `ACTIVE` and removes `BUILDING` at once (`batch()`), then removes the former version, its facts and its figures (`scan()` of `FACTS#<version>#` and `FIGURES#<version>#`, `batch()`).
- `bin/console app:reporting:rebuild` (`Reporting\Command\RebuildFiguresCommand`) calls `RebuildFiguresHandler` out of the command bus, its steps committing their own units (ADR 0103): it abandons the version an interrupted rebuild was building, waits a minute for the recordings in flight, removes every version but the active one, starts the next version, waits a minute again, counts every account again in it, then activates it (ADR 0106); a step cancelled by a recording of the worker on the same figures starts it again, three times at most (ADR 0104). It writes only the table of Reporting, without outbox; `make db` runs it after the data of the demonstration, and an instance once Reporting is put in service, after `app:keys:rebuild`: it reads the accounts in their list and the exchanges by account, keys that the items written before them lack until that command writes them, an account missing from the list being counted by no one: it says how many accounts it counted, none being no failure (a new instance has none), and `app:consistency:check` shows an account or an exchange missing from its list (`api.md`) (`make console INSTANCE=<instance> CMD="app:reporting:rebuild"`, from the command line with the AWS profile of the instance, out of the 15 minutes of a function; `infra.md`).
