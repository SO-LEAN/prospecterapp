# Persistence

What is in force; the reasons are in the records it names (`docs/adr`), read only to know why or before changing it (ADR 0090).

The aggregates live in DynamoDB, one table per bounded context (ADR 0038, 0048); the queries read their read models in the same tables, without restoring the aggregates (ADR 0088).

## Tables and items

Each bounded context has its own DynamoDB table (ADR 0038, 0048): `<DYNAMODB_TABLE_PREFIX>-<context>` (`prospecter-dev-prospecting`; the prefix `prospecter-test` in tests), a `Shared\Persistence\Table` injected by name in the persistence of its context only (`Table $prospectingTable`); `tests/Architecture/TablePerContextTest` fails otherwise. Nothing spans two contexts. Locally the tables live in MiniStack (`ministack` service of `docker-compose.yml`, `DYNAMODB_ENDPOINT`, dev credentials in `.env`); `Shared\Persistence\Tables` creates them all alike (`PK`/`SK`, the indexes `GSI1` and `GSI2`, and `NAME`, `EMAIL` and `PHONE` finding an aggregate, billed by request; on AWS `infra/tables.tf`, alike), `make db` with the data of the demonstration (`Demo\DemoData`, written through the domain and publishing its events like a use case).

- Single-table design, each repository documenting its items: `ORGANIZATION#<id>` listed by account in `GSI1` and by holding in `GSI2`, the links `CONTACT#<person>` / `ORGANIZATION#<id>`; `EXCHANGE#<id>` in the timeline of its contact in `GSI1` (`TIMELINE#<account>#<person>`, by its moment then its id, ADR 0074), the links `EXCHANGES#<organization>` / `EXCHANGE#<id>`, in the timeline of the organization in `GSI1` (`ORGANIZATION_TIMELINE#<organization>`, ADR 0076) and, while their exchange is to make conform, in `GSI2` (`TO_MAKE_CONFORM#<organization>`, counted there, ADR 0079); `PERSON#<id>` listed by account and purpose; `ACCOUNT#<id>`, `MEMBER#<id>`; the journal `JOURNAL#<subject>` (ADR 0043).
- An aggregate is stored as a plain document (`OrganizationDocument`, `PersonDocument`, `Shared\Persistence\Documents` for the value objects of the shared kernel, moments in UTC to the microsecond) and restored without its constructor by `Shared\Persistence\Hydrator`: the domain keeps its value objects and private properties (ADR 0028, 0048); a new property of an aggregate or a value object needs a change of its document.
- `save()` writes in one transaction the aggregate and what follows from it: the reservation of its email (`EMAIL#...`, written under a condition, so that a second holder cancels the transaction into `EmailAlreadyUsed`, the safety net of `EmailUniqueness`, ADR 0039, 0048), the links of its contacts.
- Lists come in slices newest first after a cursor (ADR 0049); a search finds an aggregate by the beginning of its name or of its email (ADR 0071): its item carries its search keys as the keys of the indexes `NAME` and `EMAIL`, and its phone number in E.164 as the key of `PHONE` (ADR 0072) (`Shared\Persistence\SearchIndexes`, keys only), written with it; `bin/console app:search:rebuild` writes them again for every aggregate and removes the items of the search before ADR 0071 (`TOKEN#...`).
