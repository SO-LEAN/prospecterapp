# Persistence

What is in force; the reasons are in the records it names (`docs/adr`), read only to know why or before changing it (ADR 0090).

The aggregates live in DynamoDB, one table per bounded context (ADR 0038, 0048); the queries read their read models in the same tables, without restoring the aggregates (ADR 0088).

## Tables and items

Each bounded context has its own DynamoDB table (ADR 0038, 0048): `<DYNAMODB_TABLE_PREFIX>-<context>` (`prospecter-dev-prospecting`; the prefix `prospecter-test` in tests), a `Shared\Persistence\Table` injected by name in the persistence of its context only (`Table $prospectingTable`); `tests/Architecture/TablePerContextTest` fails otherwise. Nothing spans two contexts, but the check of the consistency, which reads them all and writes none (`Consistency\ContextTables`, `api.md`, ADR 0105). Locally the tables live in MiniStack (`ministack` service of `docker-compose.yml`, `DYNAMODB_ENDPOINT`, dev credentials in `.env`); `Shared\Persistence\Tables` creates them all alike (`PK`/`SK`, the indexes `GSI1` and `GSI2`, and `NAME`, `EMAIL` and `PHONE` finding an aggregate, billed by request, their new items given to a stream (`NEW_IMAGE`) and an item forgotten past its `expiresAt`, an epoch (TTL); on AWS `infra/tables.tf`, alike), `make db` with the data of the demonstration (`Demo\DemoData`, written through the domain and publishing its events like a use case).

- Single-table design, each repository documenting its items: `ORGANIZATION#<id>` listed by account in `GSI1` and by holding in `GSI2`, the links `CONTACT#<person>` / `ORGANIZATION#<id>`; `EXCHANGE#<id>` in the timeline of its contact in `GSI1` (`TIMELINE#<account>#<person>`, by its moment then its id, ADR 0074; a cancelled exchange by the moment of its cancellation when it came first, `ExchangeKeys::positionOf()`, ADR 0112) and among those of its account in `GSI2` (`ACCOUNT_EXCHANGES#<account>`, by its id, ADR 0099), the links `EXCHANGES#<organization>` / `EXCHANGE#<id>`, in the timeline of the organization in `GSI1` (`ORGANIZATION_TIMELINE#<organization>`, ADR 0076) and, while their exchange is to make conform, in `GSI2` (`TO_MAKE_CONFORM#<organization>`, counted there, ADR 0079); while an exchange is planned, its link `AGENDA#<member>` / `EXCHANGE#<id>` in the agenda of its holder, in `GSI1` under its position (ADR 0111); whatever its status, its link `CALENDAR#<member>` / `EXCHANGE#<id>` in the calendar of its holder, who planned or noted it, in `GSI1` under the moment it happens, cancelled or not, then its id (`ExchangeKeys::calendarPositionOf()`, ADR 0115); the exchange of an e-mail imported marks it `IMPORTED_EMAIL#<exchange>` / `IMPORTED_EMAIL`, its account with it, written with the new exchange under `attribute_not_exists(PK)` (a condition carrying no refusal: the reaction is applied again, and finds it imported) and never deleted, so that an e-mail erased is not imported again (ADR 0154); `TODO#<id>` among those of its contact in `GSI1` (`CONTACT_TODOS#<account>#<person>`) and, until it is done, among those its holder still has to do in `GSI2` (`STILL_TO_DO#<member>`), the links `TODOS#<organization>` / `TODO#<id>` (ADR 0113); done or not, its link `TODO_CALENDAR#<member>` / `TODO#<id>` in the calendar of its holder, in `GSI1` under its due as written, its day, then its hour (none before any), then its id (`ToDoKeys::calendarPositionOf()`, ADR 0134), a period read between its first and its last days on the clock of its reader; a timeline read below the position of the moment of the reading for the past (`Table::query()`, the greatest first), above it for what is to come (`Table::queryAfter()`, the smallest first, `SortKeyRange`); a range of a partition between two sort keys is a key condition `BETWEEN` (`Table::queryBetween()`, the agenda in a window, the calendar in a period), never a filter after the query; `PERSON#<id>` listed by account and purpose; `ACCOUNT#<id>` listed with every account in `GSI1` (`ACCOUNTS`, by its id, ADR 0099), `MEMBER#<id>`; the versions, facts and figures of Reporting ([below](#reporting)); `OUTBOX#<commit>` / `OUTBOX`, the outbox of a commit ([below](#the-outbox)); `INBOX#<subscription>` / `<eventId>`, an event a subscription applied ([below](#the-inbox)).
- An aggregate is stored as a plain document (`OrganizationDocument`, `PersonDocument`, `Shared\Persistence\Documents` for the value objects of the shared kernel, moments in UTC to the microsecond) and restored without its constructor by `Shared\Persistence\Hydrator`: the domain keeps its value objects and private properties (ADR 0028, 0048); a new property of an aggregate or a value object needs a change of its document.
- The item of an organization, of an exchange and of a person carries its `revision`, incremented by `save()` (`Shared\Persistence\Revision`): the revision of its facts (ADR 0099), unknown to the aggregate, no optimistic lock; an item written before it reads as the revision 0 until its next save.
- `save()` writes the aggregate and what follows from it, in the unit of work of the command: the reservation of its email (`EMAIL#...`, written under a condition and carrying the refusal `EmailAlreadyUsed`, thrown when a second holder cancels the transaction: the safety net of `EmailUniqueness`, ADR 0039, 0048), the reservation of its SIRET in its account (`SIRET#<account>#<siret>` / `SIRET`, the same way, carrying `SiretAlreadyUsed`: the safety net of `SiretUniqueness`, released when it changes or the organization is no longer prospected, ADR 0158), the reservations of each of its links and of the domain of its website in its account (`REFERENCE#<account>#<source>#<identifier>` / `REFERENCE`, `WEBSITE#<account>#<domain>` / `WEBSITE`, the same way, carrying `ReferenceAlreadyUsed` and `WebsiteAlreadyUsed`: the safety nets of `ReferenceUniqueness` and `WebsiteUniqueness`, a link dropped released, written again by `app:keys:rebuild`, ADR 0160, ADR 0165), the link of its resemblance key in its account (`RESEMBLANCE#<account>#<key>` / `ORGANIZATION#<id>`, its key kept on its item as `resemblanceKey`, written when the key changes, the former link deleted, deleted by `remove()`, read by `resembling()`, written again by `app:keys:rebuild`, ADR 0162), the reservation of each link a contact holds, current or former (`REFERENCE#<account>#<source>#<identifier>` / `REFERENCE`, its `ownerId`, put under `attribute_not_exists(PK) OR ownerId = :owner` and carrying the refusal `ReferenceTakenMeanwhile`: the safety net of `ReferenceUniqueness` of PersonalData; the reservations of the links it no longer holds, and all of them when the contact is removed, read first and deleted only when the contact owns them, under `ownerId = :owner`, one another contact holds, a duplicate noted before the rule, left to them; none for the profile of a member, ADR 0159, ADR 0165), the links of its contacts.
- Lists come in slices newest first after a cursor (ADR 0049), but the lists of to-dos, a handful each, read whole (ADR 0113); a search finds an aggregate by the beginning of its name or of its email (ADR 0071), a person by the beginning of their first name too (ADR 0149): its item carries its search keys as the keys of the indexes `NAME` and `EMAIL`, the first name of a person on an item beside it (`PERSON#<id>` / `FIRST_NAME`, the keys of `NAME` only, written and removed in the transaction of the person), and its phone number in E.164 as the key of `PHONE` (ADR 0072) (`Shared\Persistence\SearchIndexes`, keys only), written with it; `bin/console app:keys:rebuild` (`Keys\RebuildKeysCommand`, composing the contexts) writes again the keys the tables derive from the aggregates, as many times as needed: the item of every organization and of every person, its search keys with it (the link of the resemblance key of an organization, ADR 0162, the item of the first name of a person beside it, ADR 0149), removing the items of the search before ADR 0071 (`TOKEN#...`), and the item of every exchange, its key by account with it, and its link in the calendar of its holder (ADR 0115), the link of every to-do in the calendar of its holder (ADR 0134), and of every account, its key in the list of the accounts with it (ADR 0099), and the reservation of every link a contact or an organization holds, one two contacts noted before it was unique for the oldest of them (ADR 0159), each link recognized again by its address, a source known since taking over the links of its host, the reservations no link holds any more deleted, the LinkedIn profiles and pages written before the links taken as links and their former reservations (`LINKEDIN#`, `LINKEDIN_PAGE#`) deleted; read, a link is restored as it was noted (ADR 0165), saying how many of each it wrote.

## The unit of work

What a command writes is kept all at once or not at all (ADR 0095), and so is what a reaction writes, in a transaction of its own after the command (ADR 0101), its inbox with it; with the delivery `sync` (the tests, an instance naming it), the reactions still join the unit of the command. `Shared\Persistence\DynamoUnitOfWork` implements the port `UnitOfWork` of the core, opened by the decorators `Transactional…Handler` of each command and reaction (`api.md`), and by the worker around a reaction (`contexts.md`); a gesture runs one use case (ADR 0100).

- While a unit is open, `Table::transact()` keeps its writes (`Write`: a put or a delete of an item, made by `putting()` and `deleting()`, a put whose condition is about the item as stored by `replacing()`, an addition to numbers of an item by `adding()`), one per item (table, PK, SK), the last one winning, except two additions, kept as one, their sum, and a `replacing()` following a put of its item, which keeps the condition of the first (ADR 0104); the outermost commit sends them in one `TransactWriteItems`, across the tables. Without an open unit (`Demo\DemoData`, the commands of the console that rebuild), a write is sent at once; `Table::batch()` is always sent at once, for a projection that can be rebuilt.
- More than 100 writes: the commit throws a `LogicException` before sending anything; a transaction is never split. Each exchange of an organization no longer prospected is erased by its own reaction (`contexts.md`), so that stopping the prospecting of an organization of many exchanges fits; with the delivery `sync`, its reactions joining the command, it is refused beyond some thirty. The contacts an organization leaves without one are forgotten in one transaction, up to eleven writes each (the person, the reservation of their email, the item of their first name, ADR 0149, the reservations of their five links at most and of their three former ones, ADR 0159, ADR 0165): eight at most, with the item of the inbox and that of the outbox.
- A conditional write carries what to throw when its condition does not hold (`EmailAlreadyUsed::byAnother(...)`, `AlreadyApplied` for an inbox, given the cancellation as its cause); the `CancellationReasons` name the write, the first one carrying something winning; the decorators of the handlers translate a refusal like any (409). A condition carrying none throws `ConditionFailed`.
- A read by key (`item()`, `items()`) and the query of a partition (`query()`, of the table or of an index) see what the open unit is about to write: an item put reads as written, an item deleted as absent, an item added to as stored. `count()`, `keysAt()`, `keysBeginningWith()` and `scan()` read the table as it was before the commit: no command reads them.
- A `discard()` without its `commit()` abandons the whole unit until its outermost level ends: Messenger still calls the other handlers of an event after one threw, and what they write is thrown away too.
- A file sent to the storage by a use case whose unit is discarded afterwards stays there, unused: the storage is outside the transaction. A file is removed from it by a reaction once its removal is written (ADR 0101, `contexts.md`).

## The outbox

With the delivery `outbox` (`INTEGRATION_EVENTS_DELIVERY=outbox`, `api.md`), what a command or a reaction publishes is written with what it changes, all or nothing, and published on the bus after the commit (ADR 0098), whose rules give it to the worker (`contexts.md`); the local stack and the instances deliver so (ADR 0108), the tests stay `sync`.

- `Shared\Messaging\OutboxDelivery` keeps each event in `Shared\Messaging\CommitOutbox`, the state of the request (`ResetInterface`), an event published again by the commit (the same source, type and payload: the facts of an aggregate, published for each of its domain events) once (ADR 0104); the outermost commit of `DynamoUnitOfWork` adds to its `TransactWriteItems` one item, in the table of the context the events happened in, named by their source, and a discarded unit throws its events away. A transaction spans one use case, hence one context: a commit publishing in two contexts throws a `LogicException` before anything is sent: no gesture does, each running one use case (ADR 0100). No event, no item. An event published outside a unit (`Demo\DemoData`) is a commit of its own.
- The item (`Shared\Persistence\OutboxItem`): PK `OUTBOX#<commit>` (a UUID v7), SK `OUTBOX`, under `attribute_not_exists(PK)`; `events` the list of the events as they travel (`{"source", "detailType", "detail"}`, `contexts.md`), a JSON string; `occurredAt` the moment of the commit (the real clock, `app.commit_clock`); `expiresAt` an epoch a week later, when the table forgets it. Its events over 400 KB: the commit throws a `LogicException` before sending anything. It counts in the 100 writes of a transaction.
- The stream of the table gives the new item to the relay (`Shared\Messaging\OutboxRelay`, `infra.md`, `local-stack.md`), which publishes its events on the bus by `Shared\Messaging\IntegrationEventBus` (`INTEGRATION_EVENT_BUS`, `EVENTBRIDGE_ENDPOINT` locally): ten entries per `PutEvents` and under 256 KB, the least a bus takes (ADR 0154), an entry refused failing the whole batch (`EventsNotPublished`), given again by the stream. It writes nothing; it logs each event published with its `eventId` and its correlation.
- `bin/console app:outbox:republish --since=<moment>` publishes again the items the tables keep, those of the commits since the moment (`Shared\Persistence\OutboxItems`): an event may be published twice, the reactions are to be idempotent.

## The inbox

A subscription whose reaction would change something applied twice (`"inbox": true`, `config/subscriptions.json`) applies an event once (ADR 0102): `Shared\Messaging\Inbox` puts, in the unit of work of the reaction, an item in the table of the context of the subscription, PK `INBOX#<subscription>`, SK the `eventId`, `receivedAt` the moment (`app.commit_clock`), `expiresAt` an epoch 15 days later, under `attribute_not_exists(SK)`; written first, its condition failing wins over the others and throws `AlreadyApplied`. It counts in the 100 writes of a transaction; a reaction that fails writes none. Two subscriptions to one event each have theirs.

## Reporting

The table of Reporting (`<prefix>-reporting`, ADR 0103, 0104) keeps figures derived from the other contexts, recalculable: no personal data, identities and booleans.

| Item | PK | SK | Attributes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active version | `PROJECTION` | `ACTIVE` | `version` |
| Version being built | `PROJECTION` | `BUILDING` | `version`, `since` |
| Fact of an organization | `FACTS#<version>#<account>` | `ORGANIZATION#<id>` | `revision`, `present`, `located` |
| Fact of an exchange | `FACTS#<version>#<account>` | `EXCHANGE#<id>` | `revision`, `present`, `held` (done or missed; absent from a fact kept before, read as held), `month` (YYYY-MM, null without a moment) |
| Fact of a contact | `FACTS#<version>#<account>` | `CONTACT#<id>` | `revision`, `present` |
| Figures of an account | `FIGURES#<version>#<account>` | `TOTAL` | `activeOrganizations`, `locatedOrganizations`, `contacts`, `exchanges` |
| Figures of a month | `FIGURES#<version>#<account>` | `MONTH#<YYYY-MM>` | `exchanges` |

- `DynamoFactRepository::record()` writes a fact in one transaction: the fact by `replacing()` under `attribute_not_exists(SK)`, or `revision = :revision AND present = :present` of the fact it replaces, read before by a consistent read; an `ADD` of the non-zero differences on `TOTAL`, and on the former and the new `MONTH#…` when the month changes. A terminal fact gets an `expiresAt` 15 days later, longer than an event may wait (6 hours in the worker, a day of retries of EventBridge, 14 days in the failures). A condition failing, or a `TransactionConflict`, cancels it (`ConditionFailed`): the worker applies the reaction again from its reads ([contexts.md](contexts.md#the-worker)). The live versions of a rebuild are written in the transaction of the reaction.
- `DynamoProjectionVersions`: `abandonBuilding()` removes `BUILDING` in its unit; `removeAbandoned()` removes every version but the active one (what an interrupted rebuild left, what a recording wrote in the former version after its removal: `scan()` of `FACTS#` and `FIGURES#`, `batch()`), once the recordings that saw the version abandoned live ended (ADR 0106); `startBuilding()` writes `BUILDING` in its unit; `activate()` writes `ACTIVE` and removes `BUILDING` at once (`batch()`), then removes the former version, its facts and its figures (`scan()` of `FACTS#<version>#` and `FIGURES#<version>#`, `batch()`).
- `bin/console app:reporting:rebuild` (`Reporting\Command\RebuildFiguresCommand`) calls `RebuildFiguresHandler` out of the command bus, its steps committing their own units (ADR 0103): it abandons the version an interrupted rebuild was building, waits a minute for the recordings in flight, removes every version but the active one, starts the next version, waits a minute again, counts every account again in it, then activates it (ADR 0106); a step cancelled by a recording of the worker on the same figures starts it again, three times at most (ADR 0104). It writes only the table of Reporting, without outbox; `make db` runs it after the data of the demonstration, and an instance once Reporting is put in service, after `app:keys:rebuild`: it reads the accounts in their list and the exchanges by account, keys that the items written before them lack until that command writes them, an account missing from the list being counted by no one: it says how many accounts it counted, none being no failure (a new instance has none), and `app:consistency:check` shows an account or an exchange missing from its list (`api.md`) (`make console INSTANCE=<instance> CMD="app:reporting:rebuild"`, from the command line with the AWS profile of the instance, out of the 15 minutes of a function; `infra.md`).

## Campaigns

The table of Campaigns (`<prefix>-campaigns`, ADR 0138) keeps the tags the members put by hand on their organizations and their contacts; the taggings of an account share its partition, read at once to propose its tags (the range `TAGGING#`). A tagging follows from the other contexts only by its removal, once its organization is no longer prospected or its contact forgotten: `app:consistency:check` reports one left behind. Beside them, the facts the tags of the contacts derive from (ADR 0141), identities and booleans: a fact replaces the one it replaces under the condition that it is still the one kept (`revision = :revision AND present = :present`, or `attribute_not_exists(SK)`), a conflict applied again from its reads; the fact of an organization writes, in its transaction, a link per contact it names that the replaced one did not, and removes those of the contacts it no longer names; a terminal fact is forgotten 15 days later (`expiresAt`). The tags of a contact are read by its keys, never the whole partition: a `Query` of the range of its fact and its links, then the taggings of the contact and of the organizations linked and the facts of those organizations, where they are (ADR 0142) (`BatchGetItem`); the geographic tags of an account, from the range of the facts of its organizations. The search of the contacts carrying every tag chosen reads the partition of the account once instead (ADR 0143): a `Query` of the range of the facts of the contacts and their links, a `Query` of the taggings, a `Query` of the facts of the organizations, where they are, the tags of each contact its fact keeps derived and filtered in the function, nothing written for it; some 400 bytes per contact, a page of 1 MB some 2,500 contacts, an account of tens of thousands of contacts the limit. Beside them too, the campaigns of the account (ADR 0144), read at once by the range `CAMPAIGN#` to list them; a draft keeps no target, read from the facts when it is shown. `bin/console app:tags:rebuild [--account=<id>]` (`Campaigns\Command\RebuildTagsCommand`) records the facts again through the same succession, each in its own unit, without version nor wait, starting again three times at most when a recording of the worker cancels it; it writes only this table, `make db` runs it.

| Item | PK | SK | Attributes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tagging | `ACCOUNT#<account>` | `TAGGING#<organization or contact>#<id>` | `tagging`: the account, the kind of what it tags (`holder`), its id (`holderId`), its tags by their names without their mark, in their order, `createdAt`, `modifiedAt`; deleted with what it tags |
| Fact of an organization | `ACCOUNT#<account>` | `FACT#ORGANIZATION#<id>` | `revision`, `present`, `contacts` (the persons it names), `municipalityCode` and `country` (where it is, ADR 0142, absent from a fact recorded before), `expiresAt` once terminal |
| Fact of a contact | `ACCOUNT#<account>` | `FACT#CONTACT#<id>` | `revision`, `present`, `hasEmail`, `hasPhone`, `hasMobile`, `hasLinkedIn`, `expiresAt` once terminal |
| Campaign | `ACCOUNT#<account>` | `CAMPAIGN#<id>` | `campaign`: its identity, the account, its author (`createdBy`), `name`, `channel`, its tags chosen by their names without their mark, `message`, `subject`, `status`, `recipients` (frozen: each `contactId` and its `status`, `pending`, `processed`, `dismissed` or `erased`, whether the call of one processed was `missed` and the `exchangeId` that processed it, the `reason` one was dismissed for, whether its contact was `erased` from a started campaign, its status then `erased` when it was still to handle and kept otherwise, without its `exchangeId` nor its `reason`, in the order added, 500 at most; none in a draft or for a campaign saved before, `missed` false and no `reason` for a recipient saved before, ADR 0145, no `exchangeId` for one processed before ADR 0152, `erased` read from the status `erased` for one saved before it was kept), `createdAt`, `modifiedAt`, `startedAt`, `stoppedAt` and `endedAt` (none before, nor for a campaign saved before); the tags it targets derived, never kept; deleted when discarded, or erased by the operations |
| Link of a contact to an organization naming it | `ACCOUNT#<account>` | `FACT#CONTACT#<id>#ORGANIZATION#<organization>` | `organization`; written and deleted with the fact of the organization |

## Booking

The table of Booking (`<prefix>-booking`, ADR 0148, 0151) keeps the booking page of each member and the link of its code to it; nothing in it follows from another context, and the check of the consistency reads none of it. A page saved with a new code puts the link of that code under `attribute_not_exists(PK)` and deletes the link of the former code, in the same transaction; the public page is read by the link of its code, then the page of its host. The limiters of its public routes keep their counts in it too, forgotten once their window passed (ADR 0153). An appointment is kept by its identity and in the partition of the slots of its host, the slot put only where none is, so that two bookings of one slot never both pass; both are forgotten 16 days after the booking, from the clock of the commit, past the last slot a link offers (ADR 0164).

| Item | PK | SK | Attributes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Booking page | `BOOKING_PAGE#<member>` | `BOOKING_PAGE` | `page`: its host, the account, `windows` (`weekday`, `from`, `to`, HH:MM), `timeZone`, `inactivePeriods` (`id`, `firstDay`, `lastDay`), `channels`, `open`, `code`, `createdAt`, `modifiedAt` |
| Link of a code | `BOOKING_CODE#<code>` | `BOOKING_CODE` | `host`: the member whose page the code leads to |
| Appointment | `APPOINTMENT#<id>` | `APPOINTMENT` | `appointment`: its id, the account, its host, `bookerId`, `slot` (a moment) and `timeZone` (the clock of the host), `channel`, `firstName`, `lastName`, `phone` (E.164), `email`, `subject`, `createdAt`; `expiresAt` |
| Slot held | `SLOTS#<host>` | `SLOT#<start>` | the same `appointment`, `expiresAt`; put under `attribute_not_exists(PK)` |
| Counts of a limiter | `RATE_LIMIT#<limiter>-<code>` | `RATE_LIMIT` | `state`: the sliding window of the limiter, serialized; `expiresAt`, the epoch the table forgets it at |
