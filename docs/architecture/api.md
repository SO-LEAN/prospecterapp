# The API

What is in force; the reasons are in the records it names (`docs/adr`), read only to know why or before changing it (ADR 0090).

`apps/api`: a JSON API exposing the use cases of the core, a resource server for the SPA (ADR 0006, 0052). Its persistence: `persistence.md`; its authentication: `auth.md`; how it runs: `local-stack.md`, `infra.md`.

## Writing an endpoint

- One business intent per endpoint (ADR 0025), one invokable controller per endpoint, in the folder of its bounded context: `src/<Context>/Controller/<Intent>Controller.php` (`Prospecting/Controller/RelocateController`, ADR 0087); `src/Me` (the `/api/me` endpoints: membership, profile), `src/Dashboard` and `src/Contacts` (a contact, `GET /api/contacts/{id}` and the intents answering it, with the link to its timeline when Prospecting offers it, `ContactReader`; `GET /api/contacts` with the organizations of each contact, `GET /api/phone-numbers/{phoneNumber}`: who calls, ADR 0072, `GET /api/contacts/{id}/organizations`, `GET /api/organizations/{id}/contacts`, and the timeline of an organization, `/api/organizations/{id}/exchanges`, its persons named by PersonalData, `OrganizationTimelineReader`, ADR 0076: one batch call per context, narrowed to its exchanges to make conform by `/api/organizations/{id}/exchanges-to-make-conform`, ADR 0079) compose contexts. `src/PersonalData/Controller` serves the forgetting of a contact, `src/Prospecting/Controller` the exchanges with a contact (`/api/contacts/{id}/exchanges`, ADR 0074, and those to come, `/api/contacts/{id}/upcoming-exchanges`, where they are planned, ADR 0111), `src/Contacts` those to come with an organization (`/api/organizations/{id}/upcoming-exchanges`) and the exchanges of the agenda of the member a slot overlaps (`GET /api/me/planned-exchanges`, `AgendaReader`), `src/Exchanges` one exchange (`/api/exchanges/{id}`: its detail, the correction of its `facts` or of its `text` alone, ADR 0078, its erasure, its `attachments`: joined, downloaded by a link signed for a moment, removed, after the browser sent the file with the form of `POST /api/uploads`, ADR 0077; `ExchangeReader` names its interlocutor, the contact in PersonalData or the organization itself in Prospecting, and the members who noted, corrected and made it conform in PersonalData, under the person ids `ConsultMembersService` of Accounts gives them, asked once for all of them); a contact is collected with its `organizationIds` (ADR 0042). Extend `AbstractController`. A change dispatches its command on `Shared\CommandBus` (synchronous, refusals of the core thrown back as is), then answers with the fresh resource read by a query (`Prospecting\OrganizationReader`, `Me\MyAccountReader`, which composes Accounts and PersonalData): commands return nothing (ADR 0029). A command is flat (ADR 0035): the controller passes the fields of the input, the id of the new resource from `Shared\Identities` (UUID v7) and `executedAt` from the Symfony clock (ADR 0036).
- URLs are resource-oriented. `POST` creates a resource or adds an entity to an aggregate (`POST /api/organizations`, `POST .../logo`), `PATCH` changes one part of a resource exposed as a sub-resource (`PATCH /api/organizations/{id}/address`), `DELETE` removes one. No `PUT`. Ids are UUIDs: routes declare `requirements: ['id' => Requirement::UUID]`.
- Input: `#[MapRequestPayload]` / `#[MapQueryString]` with a `final readonly` DTO in `src/<Context>/Input` (constraints as attributes, `#[Assert\Valid]` on nested DTOs), shaped like the matching part of the resource (`AddressInput` like `AddressResource`); the controller passes its fields to the flat command (ADR 0035). Unknown fields are rejected with a 422 violation each (`serializer.default_context` in `config/packages/framework.yaml`), never ignored.
- Files: `#[MapUploadedFile]` then `new UploadedFileInfo($file)` (an `SplFileInfo` whose extension is guessed from the content), always on `POST` (PHP does not parse multipart bodies on `PATCH`).
- Output: every intent returns the fresh resource (`OrganizationResource`, `AccountResource`), a `final readonly` class of `src/<Context>/Resource` built by a presenter of `src/<Context>/Presenter` (`src/Me/AccountResourcePresenter` for my account; the dashboard, `GET /api/dashboard`, presents the figures of Reporting, `ConsultFigures` at the moment of the call, with `Dashboard\Presenter\FiguresPresenter`, and reuses `OrganizationPagePresenter` for its markers only, a search of Prospecting of 1 000 organizations at most; a figure may lag a gesture by the delivery of its fact, about a second) from the response of a query. A presenter is an object of the call, never injected (ADR 0085): it implements the output port of its query, the controller or the reader (`OrganizationReader`, `TimelineReader`, `ExchangeReader`, `MyAccountReader`, `ContactReader`, `OrganizationTimelineReader`) builds it with the services it needs and the answers read before, passes it to the query, then answers with its `resource`; the other answers are read with the presenters of the core keeping them (`KeptPersons`), and a stateless mapper is a factory (`TimelineResourceFactory`, `ContactInListResourceFactory`, and for the images `Shared\ImageResourceFactory`: `url`, `smallUrl` 96 px and `mediumUrl` 256 px wide, made on first request by the storage app, never enlarged; the front picks the size it displays). Inputs live in `src/<Context>/Input`; what several contexts use in `src/Shared/{Input,Resource}`.
- Document every endpoint: `#[OA\Tag]`, `#[OA\Get|Post|Patch|Delete]`(summary), one `#[OA\Response]` per status with `new Model(type: ...)`. Then run `make api-doc` to regenerate `openapi.json` and the front types; `tests/Api/OpenApiContractTest` fails `make test` while the committed `openapi.json` differs from the code.
- Roles: one `#[IsGranted('ROLE_PROSPECTOR')]` or `#[IsGranted('ROLE_USER')]` per controller (`ROLE_ADMIN` inherits both, and `ROLE_PERSONAL_DATA`: an administrator manages the personal data the core lets them read, `Member::readsPersonalData()`); the core does not check roles.
- Access to a resource of Prospecting (ADR 0081): the controller receives its read model, an `OrganizationSheet` or an `ExchangeDetail` resolved from the `id` of the route (`Prospecting\OrganizationResolver`, `Exchanges\ExchangeResolver`), and declares `#[IsGranted(OrganizationVoter::ACCESS, subject: 'organization')]` (or `ExchangeVoter::ACCESS`; `tests/Architecture/ResourcesAreReachedThroughTheirVoterTest` fails otherwise). The voter (`Security\AccountResourceVoter`) only refuses a resource of another account, a 404 named like the refusal of the core (`unknownOrganization`, by `Security\ApiAccessDeniedHandler`); an intention needing the right to manage personal data declares the role too, `#[IsGranted(User::PERSONAL_DATA)]` (403), `StopProspecting` only while the organization has contacts (`denyAccessUnlessGranted()`); the state is checked by the core (409). The core still checks a contact of the account and what PersonalData lets its reader do.
- Links: a resource carries `_links` (HAL, plus `method`), built by its presenter from the possible intents of the core response (`Shared\Links`), those of Prospecting shown only when the member holds the role their controller requires, declared beside the route in the table of the presenter (`Security\IntentLinks`, ADR 0081, 0088): the key of a link is absent when the intention is not possible now (`SKIP_NULL_VALUES` on `_links`, optional in the contract), and the call then fails. Document a new intention as a new link.
- Errors: a use case refuses with `UseCaseRefused`, which `Shared\UseCaseExceptionListener` turns into RFC 9457 `application/problem+json` by its `ErrorType`: `NotFound` 404, `NotPossibleInItsState` 409 (the link is missing), `NotAllowed` 403 (no right to manage personal data in PersonalData: search contacts by text, collect or forget a contact; download an attachment; the profile of a colleague; in Prospecting the voters refuse the rest first), `BusinessRuleViolated` 422 with `violations`; the problem and its violation carry the `reason` (the class of the refusal, `invalidPhoneNumber`) and its `context`, which the SPA words in French (ADR 0073); any other exception is a failure (500); missing tokens 401 (`Security\ApiAuthenticationEntryPoint`), missing role or right 403, a resource of another account 404 (`Security\ApiAccessDeniedHandler`).
- CORS (`config/packages/nelmio_cors.yaml`) lists the allowed methods: a new verb must be added there, or the browser's preflight fails, and in the CORS of the HTTP API of `docker/lambda/deploy.sh` (MiniStack answers the preflights of the Lambda mode from it). In dev only, both expose `X-Debug-Token` and `X-Debug-Token-Link` too, so that the front reads the profile of a call (`tests/Local/ProfilerTest` checks they agree). On AWS the allowed origin, `CORS_ALLOW_ORIGIN`, is made by its Terraform from the address of the front (ADR 0066, 0067).

## The delivery and the wiring

The delivery (controllers, inputs, resources, presenters, readers, security) uses the requests and responses of the application only; only the adapters of the ports use the domain (`tests/Architecture/DomainBehindTheApplicationTest`, ADR 0035). A context of the API uses nothing of another one but its Contract (`<Context>/Contract` of the core: published queries and integration events, `tests/Architecture/BoundedContextsTest`, ADR 0045, 0047). Security reads who calls with the query `ConsultMyAccount` (identities and roles, ADR 0040). Repositories answer the questions of the domain (`isEmailUsed()`) and decide nothing; the reservation of the email written with the aggregate (below) is the safety net of `EmailUniqueness` when two requests take the same email at once (ADR 0039, 0048). `tests/Architecture/BoundedContextsTest` fails when a context folder uses anything of another context but its Contract, the anticorruption adapters included.

The adapters of a context are grouped by kind: its repositories in `src/<Context>/Persistence`, the anticorruption layers of its ports to the other contexts in `src/<Context>/AntiCorruption` (ADR 0086). A console command using the adapters of several contexts has a folder of its own, `src/<Purpose>` (`src/Keys`, `src/Consistency`), never `src/Shared`, which uses no context (`tests/Architecture/BoundedContextsTest`). Ports are aliased to adapters in `config/services.yaml`. Every `*Handler` of the core is a service, tagged by its interface and decorated by `Shared\DependencyInjection\RefusalTranslatingHandlersPass` with the decorators of its kind, from the innermost to the outermost (`COMMAND_DECORATORS`, `EVENT_DECORATORS`, `QUERY_DECORATORS`, ADR 0094, 0095): those implementing `Prospecter\Core\SharedKernel\Application\CommandHandler` are registered on `command.bus` through `TransactionalCommandHandler` then `RefusalTranslatingCommandHandler`, those implementing `EventHandler` through `TransactionalEventHandler` then `RefusalTranslatingEventHandler`, tagged with the id of the handler they decorate as `alias` (Messenger calls a handler of a given name once per message, its parent classes included: decorated alike, the translator of `ContactsLeftWithoutOrganization` and that of its parent `OrganizationEvent` would be one): a translator (`Application/Publish…/Publish…Handler`) on `event.bus`, any other taken off it by `Shared\DependencyInjection\SubscriptionsPass` and reached as the subscription naming it in `config/subscriptions.json` (`Shared\Messaging\Subscriptions`, `contexts.md`), a reaction no subscription names failing the build, a subscription naming one reaction (`handler`) or one per event it listens to (`handlers`), reached by its name and the detail-type of the event (`Subscriptions::reactionKey()`, ADR 0104); the commands and the translators routed by the type of the message their handler takes; the same pass wraps every `QueryHandler` in a `RefusalTranslatingQueryHandler` and aliases it by the name of its intent, so a class calling one asks for the interface of the core, `QueryHandler $consultOrganization` (`ConsultOrganizationHandler`), never for the decorator (ADR 0035, 0044, 0094); `RebuildFiguresHandler` alone is no service of the bus: its steps commit their own units, `app:reporting:rebuild` calls it (ADR 0103, 0104); the services the contexts offer each other are aliased by their interface to the handler implementing it, and asked for by their type (`ConsultMembersService $consultMembersService`, ADR 0089). Nothing is built in a constructor and no service by the code: everything is injected (`make lint` checks it, ADR 0044); the services of the hexagon are registered in `config/services.yaml` (`config/packages/messenger.yaml`: no transport, handled in the request). The port `Events` is `Shared\Messaging\RoutingEvents` (ADR 0097): a domain event goes to the translators of its context on `event.bus` (synchronous, `allow_no_handlers`), then, like an integration event, encoded (`contexts.md`) to the `Shared\Messaging\Delivery` that `INTEGRATION_EVENTS_DELIVERY` names: `sync`, `SynchronousDelivery`, calling at once the reactions of the subscriptions listening to its source and detail-type, internal ones included, without inbox, in no promised order (ADR 0097); or `outbox`, `OutboxDelivery`, writing it in the outbox of the commit, relayed to the bus after it, no reaction running within the command (ADR 0098, `persistence.md`), the delivery of the local stack and of the instances (ADR 0108). The reactions take a domain event of their context or an integration event of another context (ADR 0042, 0046); with the delivery `sync` (the tests, an instance naming it), they join the unit of work of the command that published, a reaction refused or failing refusing or failing the command, which writes nothing (`persistence.md`, ADR 0095); with `outbox`, the worker runs each in its own transaction (`contexts.md`, ADR 0101). A gesture runs one use case: the commands of the console opening an account or registering a member dispatch `OpenAccount` or `RegisterAMember` alone, the member completing their profile in the SPA (ADR 0100). `bin/console debug:messenger` (`make commands`) lists both buses.

## Checking the consistency

`bin/console app:consistency:check` (`--account=<id>`: one account; `make console CMD="app:consistency:check"`, with `INSTANCE=` on an instance) checks the result of the asynchronous reactions rather than watching their failures (ADR 0105), on demand, reading only:

- **the figures**: the five figures of each account (`activeOrganizations`, `locatedOrganizations`, `contacts`, `exchanges`, and the exchanges of each month) counted again from the tables of Prospecting and PersonalData (a `Query` counting on `GSI1` and `GSI2`, the items read for those placed on the map and the months), against the active version of Reporting;
- **the reactions between the contexts**: each account has members, each member their profile in PersonalData, each contact collected is attached to organizations of its account, no contact forgotten still is, no exchange is kept for an organization no longer prospected, nor for none;
- **the lists**: each account has the key listing it among the accounts (`GSI1`, `ACCOUNTS`) and each exchange the key listing it in its account (`GSI2`), by which `app:reporting:rebuild` counts them: an item written before them lacks them, and nothing counts it until `app:keys:rebuild` writes them. The check finds the accounts by their items (a `Scan` of `ACCOUNT#`), never by that list, so that it checks too an account missing from it, and the exchanges lacking their key by a `Scan` of `EXCHANGE#`; the figures and the reactions still read the exchanges of an account by `GSI2`: when an item is missing from its list, run `app:keys:rebuild` first, then `app:reporting:rebuild`, then check again;
- **the queues of the failures**: the messages waiting, visible or not, in those of the worker (`INTEGRATION_FAILURES_QUEUE`) and of the relay (`RELAY_FAILURES_QUEUE`), whatever the account.

It lists each discrepancy with its account, what it concerns (a figure, an identity, a queue) and the command correcting it, and fails when there is one: a figure is corrected by `app:reporting:rebuild`; an item missing from its list by `app:keys:rebuild`; a reaction missed by `app:integration:redrive` when the failures of the worker keep its event, otherwise by `app:outbox:republish --since=<moment of the gesture>`; a failure of the relay by `app:outbox:republish`. It corrects nothing. It counts by direct requests on the tables, never through the services the contexts publish nor their retrievers, which would hide their own bugs: `Consistency\ContextTables` is the only code reading the tables of several contexts, and only reads them (`tests/Architecture/TablePerContextTest`).

## Hosts

- `APP_API_HOST` (`api.local.prospecter.io`), routes in `src/{Me,Prospecting,PersonalData,SignIn,Dashboard,Contacts,Exchanges}/Controller` (`config/routes.yaml`), and `src/Doc` (`/favicon.ico`, `config/routes/nelmio_api_doc.yaml`; `VersionedNelmioAssets` asks the files of Swagger UI on the CDN at the installed version of NelmioApiDocBundle); locally nginx passes `/cognito/` of the same host to the Cognito of MiniStack, for the SPA.
- Files are not the API's business: the storage app (`apps/storage`, `static.local.prospecter.io`) keeps and serves them, and `Shared\ImageResourceFactory` names their sizes by its convention (`<public url>/<size>/<path>`, ADR 0033).
