# The API

This document states the rules in force. The decision records (`docs/adr`) explain why: read them only for that, or before changing a rule (ADR 0090).

`apps/api` is a JSON API that exposes the core's use cases and acts as a resource server for the SPA (ADR 0006, 0052). Its persistence: `persistence.md`. Its authentication: `auth.md`. How it runs: `local-stack.md`, `infra.md`.

## Writing an endpoint

- One business intent per endpoint (ADR 0025), and one invokable controller per endpoint, in its bounded context's folder: `src/<Context>/Controller/<Intent>Controller.php` (`Prospecting/Controller/RelocateController`, ADR 0087). `src/Me`, `src/Connectors`, `src/Dashboard` and `src/Contacts` compose contexts:
  - `src/Me`: the `/api/me` endpoints: membership, profile, reminders (`/api/me/reminders`).
  - `src/Connectors`: the member's connections (ADR 0120).
    - `GET /api/me/connections` returns them in their last known state; `POST /api/me/connections/checks` asks their providers.
    - `POST /api/me/connections/google/authorizations` returns the Google address, plus the `state` and the verifier that `Connectors\Google\GoogleAuthorization` generates, for the Google client of the member's account. It is refused while the account has no client (`providerUnavailable`, 409; the `connectGoogle` and `reconnect` links are then missing). `POST /api/me/connections/google` passes it the code Google sent the SPA back with. `DELETE /api/me/connections/google` disconnects.
    - Each connection counts the member's upcoming exchanges that are `synced` and `failed` in their calendar. `POST /api/me/connections/google/retries` pushes all the failed ones again at once (link `retryFailed`, while one failed).
    - `GET /api/exchanges/{id}/google-sync` says where an exchange's push into the member's calendar stands (`sync`: `pending`, `synced` or `failed`, its moment `at` and its `reason`, `calendarRefusedThePush` or `toReconnect`; none for another member's exchange, without a connection, or for a past exchange never pushed). Its holder pushes it again with `POST /api/exchanges/{id}/google-sync/retries` (link `retry`, once Google refused it; `notTheHolder` otherwise), ADR 0133.
    - The Google client of an administrator's account (ADR 0125): `GET`, `PATCH` and `DELETE /api/me/organization/clients/google`, `ROLE_ADMIN`. Its secret is never returned. The connections link it as `googleClient`.
    - Gmail e-mail import (ADR 0154):
      - The client returns `gmailNotificationEndpoint`, where the account's subscription pushes, and `gmailNotifications` (`topic`, `pushAccount`) once its administrator has set them. `PATCH` and `DELETE /api/me/organization/clients/google/gmail-notifications` change them (links `configureGmailNotifications`, while the account has a client, and `removeGmailNotifications`; `invalidTopic` 422).
      - Each connection returns `gmailImport` (`off`, `active`, `toReconnect`) and `lastGmailNotificationAt`.
      - `POST /api/me/connections/google/gmail-import/authorizations` asks for the import (`ROLE_PERSONAL_DATA`; link `importGmail` while the account has a topic and the import is not active; `mailImportUnavailable`, 409, without a topic). It returns the Google address that asks for `gmail.readonly` along with the calendar, by incremental consent. Its code goes to `POST /api/me/connections/google`, as for a connection. `DELETE /api/me/connections/google/gmail-import` stops it (link `stopGmailImport`, while it is not off).
      - `POST /api/contacts/{id}/gmail-catch-ups` and `POST /api/organizations/{id}/gmail-catch-ups` catch up the e-mails already exchanged with a contact, or with an organization itself (the second goes through the organizations' voter). Both need `ROLE_PERSONAL_DATA` and return 202 without a body; the import records the e-mails a moment later. The `catchUpGmail` link appears on the contact and on the organization's timeline while Connectors offers it (`ConsultMailCatchUpIntents`). Refusals: `mailImportNotActive` and `noAddressToCatchUp`, 409; `unknownContact` and `unknownOrganization`, 404 (ADR 0176).
      - Pub/Sub pushes its notifications to `POST /api/accounts/{account}/gmail-notifications` (204), behind its own firewall (`auth.md`).
      - `app:gmail:renew-watches` renews the watch of each active import. It is one of the tasks an instance's daily schedule runs; `app:scheduled-tasks:run` runs them all (ADR 0161).
  - `src/Dashboard`.
  - `src/Contacts`:
    - a contact, `GET /api/contacts/{id}`, with the intents it offers, the link to its timeline when Prospecting offers it, and the Gmail catch-up when Connectors does (`ContactReader`);
    - `GET /api/contacts`, with each contact's organizations and every tag it carries (`TagsOfContacts`, ADR 0141). It links `searchByTags` for every prospector: `GET /api/tagged-contacts?tags=ESN,EMAIL` returns the contacts carrying the 1 to 5 chosen tags (their names without their mark). Campaigns finds and counts them (`found`); PersonalData reads them in clear or masked, with their organizations and tags, newest first, after a cursor. An invalid selection is refused with `invalidTagSelection` or `invalidTag` (ADR 0143);
    - `GET /api/phone-numbers/{phoneNumber}`: who is calling (ADR 0072);
    - `GET /api/contacts/{id}/organizations` and `GET /api/organizations/{id}/contacts`;
    - an organization's timeline, `/api/organizations/{id}/exchanges`. PersonalData names its persons, and the Gmail catch-up is linked when Connectors offers it (`OrganizationTimelineReader`, ADR 0076: one batch call per context). `/api/organizations/{id}/exchanges-to-make-conform` narrows it to the exchanges to redact (ADR 0079);
    - the upcoming exchanges with an organization (`/api/organizations/{id}/upcoming-exchanges`);
    - the exchanges in the member's agenda that a slot overlaps (`GET /api/me/planned-exchanges`, `AgendaReader`);
    - the member's calendar (`GET /api/me/calendar?from=&to=`, `to` excluded, ADR 0115, 0119): the exchanges they hold over a period of six weeks at most, whatever their status. Each exchange they may move at the time of reading carries its `move` link, and the `searchContacts` link lets them plan in a free slot; every prospector plans (ADR 0129);
    - the member's day on the dashboard (`GET /api/me/day?from=&to=`): what they still have planned on the day the browser gives, then the next five. Each exchange comes with its contact's organization, read in one batch. Those whose time has come carry the `markDone` and `markMissed` links; `searchContacts` works as in the calendar;
    - the exchanges the member has to process (`GET /api/me/exchanges-to-process`): planned, ended and left unhandled, oldest first, whatever their day. Each comes with its contact's organization and the `markDone`, `markMissed`, `move` and `cancel` links its state offers.

    These agenda readings leave out an exchange held with an organization alone when stopping prospecting erased that organization before the exchange, while the reaction that erases the exchange has not run yet (`InterlocutorResource::known()`).
  - `src/PersonalData/Controller`: forgetting a contact.
  - `src/Prospecting/Controller`: the exchanges with a contact (`/api/contacts/{id}/exchanges`, ADR 0074), the upcoming ones (`/api/contacts/{id}/upcoming-exchanges`, where they are planned, ADR 0111), and the place proposed to meet the contact (`/api/contacts/{id}/meeting-place`).
  - `src/ToDos`: the to-dos (ADR 0113). A contact's to-dos (`/api/contacts/{id}/to-dos`) and those of an organization and its contacts (`/api/organizations/{id}/to-dos`), created there; those the member still has to do (`/api/me/to-dos`); one to-do (`/api/to-dos/{id}`), revised and erased, ticked off and unticked with `POST` and `DELETE` on `/api/to-dos/{id}/done`. Every gesture needs the right to manage personal data. `ToDoReader` names their holders and their contacts through PersonalData; `ToDoResolver` and `ToDoVoter` reach a to-do.
  - `src/Tags`: the tags Campaigns keeps (ADR 0138):
    - an organization's tags, `GET` and `POST /api/organizations/{id}/tags`, through its voter (`TagsReader`);
    - a contact's tags, `/api/contacts/{id}/tags` (the core checks the contact belongs to the account): its own tags, the tags it inherits along with the organizations they come from, and the automatic ones (ADR 0141, `ContactTagsReader`);
    - `DELETE .../tags/{tag}` removes an own tag (its name without its mark); every prospector can tag;
    - the tags proposed to the account, `GET /api/tags`: the predefined ones by family, then the account's own, and separately the automatic ones (`automatic`), to search contacts by. The cards link them as `tags`.
  - `src/Campaigning`: the team's campaigns, which Campaigns keeps, their authors named by Accounts and PersonalData (ADR 0144):
    - `GET` and `POST /api/campaigns`: the whole list, each campaign with its target counted and its `progress`, that is its recipients `processed` and `dismissed` (none before it starts);
    - `GET`, `PATCH` and `DELETE /api/campaigns/{id}`: a draft is read, revised as a whole, or discarded. Its `target` link is the search by its targeted tags, `/api/tagged-contacts`. `POST /api/campaigns/{id}/freezing` freezes it (link `freeze`);
    - once frozen, its name and its message can still be revised. Its `recipients` link leads to `GET /api/campaigns/{id}/recipients`: every recipient, read in full the way the tag search shows its contacts (`RecipientsReader`). Each recipient has its `status` (`pending`, `processed`, `dismissed`, `erased`), whether its call was `missed`, its dismissal `reason`, and its `remove` link, `DELETE /api/campaigns/{id}/recipients/{contactId}`. `POST /api/campaigns/{id}/recipients` adds a chosen contact (link `addRecipient`), and `POST /api/campaigns/{id}/recipients/by-tags` adds the contacts of a search (link `addRecipientsByTags`). Each returns the campaign, its recipients counted as `targeted`;
    - `POST /api/campaigns/{id}/starting` starts it (link `start`, once frozen and while it keeps a recipient; a frozen campaign that its contacts' erasure emptied is refused with `campaignWithoutRecipient`, 422). `POST /api/campaigns/{id}/stopping` stops it (link `stop`). Each returns the campaign with its `startedAt` and its `stoppedAt`. A started campaign refuses any other gesture (`startedCampaign`, `startedCampaignNotDiscarded`, 409). It shows a recipient that PersonalData erased without its `contact`, with its status: `erased` when the recipient was still to handle; otherwise its `processed`, `missed` or `dismissed` is kept, without its `reason`;
    - a recipient still to handle in a started campaign links `noteExchange`, `POST /api/campaigns/{id}/recipients/{contactId}/exchanges`. That is Prospecting's controller, `src/Prospecting/Controller/NoteCampaignExchangeController`, without the campaigns' voter: the core refuses another account's campaign (`unknownCampaign`). It returns 201, the contact's timeline and the exchange's `Location`. The recipient also links `dismiss`, `POST /api/campaigns/{id}/recipients/{contactId}/dismissal` (204). Both are refused once the campaign no longer runs (`campaignNotRunning`) or once the recipient is handled (`recipientAlreadyProcessed`, 409), ADR 0145;
    - a processed recipient links the exchange logged for them, `exchange` (`GET /api/exchanges/{id}`), ADR 0152;
    - once ended (`endedAt`) or stopped, a campaign links only itself and its recipients;
    - an exchange in a timeline and an exchange's detail name its `campaign` (`id`, `name`; none once the campaign is erased, none for an exchange outside any campaign). The detail links it as `campaign` for a member with the right to manage personal data, while the campaign is kept (ADR 0152);
    - an exchange in a timeline and an exchange's detail name the provider of the mailbox it was imported from, `importedFrom` (`google`); none for an exchange logged by hand. The detail links its Gmail message, `openInMailbox` (`https://mail.google.com/mail/#all/<id>`, opened in a new tab), for a member with the right to manage personal data, once the message's mailbox id is kept (ADR 0174);
    - only operations erase a campaign, whatever its status: `bin/console app:campaign:erase <id>`;
    - `GET /api/campaigns/{id}/messages/{contactId}`: the campaign's message, rendered by the core for a contact.

    Every gesture needs the right to manage personal data. A campaign is reached through `CampaignResolver` and `CampaignVoter`. My account links the campaigns as `campaigns`.
  - `src/Booking`: the member's booking page (ADR 0148, 0151):
    - `GET /api/me/booking-page?timeZone=` (leaving out its inactive periods that ended before the reader's day), `PATCH .../windows` (all at once, with the browser's time zone), `POST .../inactive-periods` (201) and `DELETE .../inactive-periods/{id}` (each period's `remove` link), `PATCH .../channels`, `POST` and `DELETE .../opening` (links `open` and `close`, as its state offers them; opening is refused without a connected calendar, `noConnectedCalendar`, or without a channel, `noChannelOffered`, 422), and `POST .../link` (link `changeLink`, once it has one). Each returns the page. Every member manages their own (`ROLE_USER`). `Booking\BookingCodes` generates the link's code.
    - The page a link leads to, `GET /api/booking-pages/{code}`, is read without signing in (`auth.md`): who the host is, their channels, their time zone, and the free slots over the 14 days from tomorrow. It gives the same empty answer for a code that no page has now (whatever its shape, a truncated one too), for an oversized code (beyond 64 characters: the limiter does not count it and the table is not searched for it, since its keys hold 2 KB at most), and for a closed or suspended page (ADR 0153). The answer is cached for 30 seconds (`Cache-Control: public, max-age=30`).
    - Every public Booking route is rate limited by its link's code. A route says so with its default `_limited_by_its_code` (`Booking\PublicBookingRoutes`, the `public_booking_link` limiter of `config/packages/rate_limiter.yaml`, 30 a minute, its counts in Booking's table, `persistence.md`). Beyond the limit, it answers 429 `tooManyRequests` with `Retry-After`, which nobody caches. Rate limiting by address is the job of the installation's edge, not of the app, which never reads the client's address.
  - `src/Exchanges`: one exchange (`/api/exchanges/{id}`):
    - its detail;
    - the correction of its `facts` or of its `text` alone (ADR 0078), or of its `venue`, a meeting's place or a video call's link (ADR 0114);
    - the number its interlocutor is called at, when read in clear (never without the right to manage personal data, ADR 0129);
    - a planned exchange marked `done` with its report, or `missed`;
    - its `moment` moved, with a new duration if one is given (ADR 0119); the response links `moveBack` while the moment it was moved from is still to come (ADR 0135);
    - its `cancellation` by its holder (ADR 0112);
    - its erasure;
    - its `attachments`: attached, downloaded through a link signed for a short time, removed, after the browser sent the file with the `POST /api/uploads` form (ADR 0077).

    `ExchangeReader` names its interlocutor (the contact, through PersonalData, or the organization itself, through Prospecting) and, through PersonalData, the members who logged, corrected and redacted it, under the person ids that Accounts' `ConsultMembersService` gives them, asked once for all of them.
  - A contact is collected with its `organizationIds` (ADR 0042).
- Controllers extend `AbstractController`. A change dispatches its command on `Shared\CommandBus` (synchronous; the core's refusals are thrown back as they are), then returns the fresh resource, read by a query (`Prospecting\OrganizationReader`, `Me\MyAccountReader`, which composes Accounts and PersonalData): commands return nothing (ADR 0029). A command is flat (ADR 0035): the controller passes the input's fields, the new resource's id from `Shared\Identities` (UUID v7), and `executedAt` from the Symfony clock (ADR 0036).
- `src/Identification` also composes Prospecting and PersonalData, without an endpoint: it finds which persons and organizations some clues point to, for the MCP tool `identify` (`IdentificationReader`, ADR 0173), and records a contact and their organization in one call, for the MCP tool `record-contact` (`ContactRecorder`, ADR 0177).
- URLs are resource-oriented. `POST` creates a resource or adds an entity to an aggregate (`POST /api/organizations`, `POST .../logo`). `PATCH` changes one part of a resource, exposed as a sub-resource (`PATCH /api/organizations/{id}/address`). `DELETE` removes one. No `PUT`. Ids are UUIDs: routes declare `requirements: ['id' => Requirement::UUID]`.
- Input: `#[MapRequestPayload]` / `#[MapQueryString]` with a `final readonly` DTO in `src/<Context>/Input` (constraints as attributes, `#[Assert\Valid]` on nested DTOs), shaped like the matching part of the resource (`AddressInput` like `AddressResource`). The controller passes its fields to the flat command (ADR 0035). Unknown fields are rejected with one 422 violation each (`serializer.default_context` in `config/packages/framework.yaml`), never ignored: one query string is one DTO. A reading that depends on its reader's day or hour (what is late, what is on today) takes the `timeZone` query parameter, a time zone database name (`Assert\Timezone`, 422 otherwise). The controller sets the server clock in that zone before giving the moment to the query: for a period, the offset of `from` without it (`CalendarQuery`); otherwise UTC (`ReaderClockQuery`). A day without an hour is never converted to an instant (ADR 0134).
- Files: `#[MapUploadedFile]`, then `new UploadedFileInfo($file)` (an `SplFileInfo` whose extension is guessed from the content), always on `POST` (PHP does not parse multipart bodies on `PATCH`).
- Output: every intent returns the fresh resource (`OrganizationResource`, `AccountResource`), a `final readonly` class of `src/<Context>/Resource`, built from a query's response by a presenter of `src/<Context>/Presenter`. `src/Me/AccountResourcePresenter` presents my account. The dashboard, `GET /api/dashboard`, presents Reporting's figures (`ConsultFigures` at the time of the call, with `Dashboard\Presenter\FiguresPresenter`) and reuses `OrganizationPagePresenter` for its markers only, a Prospecting search of 1 000 organizations at most; a figure may lag behind a gesture by the time its fact takes to be delivered, about a second.
  - A presenter is an object of the call, never injected (ADR 0085). It implements its query's output port. The controller or the reader builds it with the services it needs and the answers read before, passes it to the query, then returns its `resource`. The readers are `OrganizationReader`, `TimelineReader`, `ExchangeReader`, `MyAccountReader`, `ContactReader`, `OrganizationTimelineReader`, and those composing several queries, shared with the MCP server's tools: `OrganizationSearchReader`, `ContactSearchReader`, `TaggedContactsReader`, `OrganizationContactsReader`, `CallerReader`, `DashboardReader`.
  - The other answers are read with the core's presenters that keep them (`KeptPersons`).
  - A stateless mapper is a factory (`TimelineResourceFactory`, `ContactInListResourceFactory`, and for images `Shared\ImageResourceFactory`: `url`, `smallUrl` 96 px wide and `mediumUrl` 256 px wide, made by the storage app on first request, never enlarged; the front picks the size it displays).
  - Inputs live in `src/<Context>/Input`; what several contexts use lives in `src/Shared/{Input,Resource}`.
- A member's endpoint comes with its MCP server tool, next to its controller (`src/<Context>/Mcp/<Intent>Tool.php`, `#[Adapts]` naming its route), or it is listed in `tests/Architecture/EveryEndpointHasItsToolTest` (`NEVER`: kept on the screen; `TO_COVER`: the writes still to cover). The test fails otherwise (ADR 0163, `mcp.md`).
- Document every endpoint: `#[OA\Tag]`, `#[OA\Get|Post|Patch|Delete]` (summary), and one `#[OA\Response]` per status with `new Model(type: ...)`. Then run `make api-doc` to regenerate `openapi.json` and the front types. `tests/Api/OpenApiContractTest` fails `make test` while the committed `openapi.json` differs from the code.
- Roles: one `#[IsGranted('ROLE_PROSPECTOR')]` or `#[IsGranted('ROLE_USER')]` per controller. `ROLE_ADMIN` inherits both, plus `ROLE_PERSONAL_DATA`: an administrator manages the personal data the core lets them read (`Member::readsPersonalData()`). The core does not check roles.
- Access to a Prospecting resource (ADR 0081): the controller receives its read model, an `OrganizationSheet` or an `ExchangeDetail` resolved from the route's `id` (`Prospecting\OrganizationResolver`, `Exchanges\ExchangeResolver`), and declares `#[IsGranted(OrganizationVoter::ACCESS, subject: 'organization')]` (or `ExchangeVoter::ACCESS`; `tests/Architecture/ResourcesAreReachedThroughTheirVoterTest` fails otherwise).
  - The voter (`Security\AccountResourceVoter`) only refuses another account's resource, with a 404 named like the core's refusal (`unknownOrganization`, through `Security\ApiAccessDeniedHandler`).
  - An intent that needs the right to manage personal data also declares the role, `#[IsGranted(User::PERSONAL_DATA)]` (403). `StopProspecting` needs it only while the organization has contacts (`denyAccessUnlessGranted()`).
  - The core checks the state (409). It still checks that a contact belongs to the account, and what PersonalData lets its reader do.
- Links: a resource carries `_links` (HAL, plus `method`), built by its presenter from the possible intents in the core's response (`Shared\Links`). Prospecting's links appear only when the member holds the role their controller requires, declared next to the route in the presenter's table (`Security\IntentLinks`, ADR 0081, 0088). A link's key is absent when the intent is not possible right now (`SKIP_NULL_VALUES` on `_links`, optional in the contract), and the call would then fail. Document a new intent as a new link.
- Errors: a use case refuses with `UseCaseRefused`, which `Shared\UseCaseExceptionListener` turns into an RFC 9457 `application/problem+json` according to its `ErrorType`:
  - `NotFound`: 404;
  - `NotPossibleInItsState`: 409 (the link is missing);
  - `NotAllowed`: 403. In PersonalData, no right to manage personal data: recognize a caller by their number, collect or forget a contact, download an attachment, a colleague's profile. In Prospecting, a planned exchange handled by a member other than its holder (`notTheHolder`, ADR 0112). In Connectors, a code the provider refuses (`accessNotGranted`, ADR 0120), and an account's client touched by a member who is not an administrator (`notTheAdministrator`, ADR 0125). The voters refuse the rest first;
  - `BusinessRuleViolated`: 422 with `violations`.

  A write to the same organization, person or exchange may cancel a command's transaction after the command read it (`ConditionFailed`, ADR 0181). `Shared\CommandBus` then runs the command again from its reads when the core marks it as an addition (`Addition`: `CollectContact`, `AddContact`; not `AttachFile`, whose upload the storage keeps before the commit, and only once), three times at most, after a random wait under 100 ms. Otherwise, or if it is still cancelled, it is refused with `ChangedMeanwhile` (409 `changedMeanwhile`: the member or the AI reads the record again).

  The problem and its violation carry the `reason` (the refusal's class, `invalidPhoneNumber`) and its `context`, which the SPA words in French (ADR 0073).

  An AWS service out of reach is a `Shared\ServiceUnavailable`, which `Shared\ServiceUnavailableListener` answers with a 503 and its `reason`, once Symfony has logged its cause:
  - `secretsUnavailable`: SSM refuses or fails to put, read or delete an account's secret, thrown by `Shared\Persistence\AccountSecrets`;
  - `googleUnreachable`: Google does not answer a code exchange or a connections check (a timeout, a 5xx or a 429), thrown by `Connectors\Google\GoogleProviderAccess`. Nothing gets connected, the connections stay as last seen, and the disconnection goes on without Google;
  - `directoryUnreachable`: the company directory fails, rate limits, answers no JSON, or does not answer a search for an organization's establishments, thrown by `Prospecting\Directory\RechercheEntreprisesDirectory`. The SIRET can still be typed by hand (ADR 0158);
  - `privateFilesUnavailable`: the private bucket fails (5xx), rate limits (429) or does not answer a file the API sends for an AI; any other refusal of the form is a failure. Thrown by `Shared\UploadsSentByTheApi` (ADR 0172).

  Any other exception is a failure (500). A missing token gets a 401 (`Security\ApiAuthenticationEntryPoint`); a missing role or right a 403 with the `reason` `roleNotAllowed`; another account's resource a 404 (`Security\ApiAccessDeniedHandler`).
- CORS (`config/packages/nelmio_cors.yaml`) lists the allowed methods. A new verb must be added there, or the browser's preflight fails, and also to the HTTP API's CORS in `docker/lambda/deploy.sh` (MiniStack answers the preflights of the Lambda mode from it). In dev only, both also expose `X-Debug-Token` and `X-Debug-Token-Link`, so that the front can read a call's profile (`tests/Local/ProfilerTest` checks that they agree). On AWS, the API's Terraform derives the allowed origin, `CORS_ALLOW_ORIGIN`, from the front's address (ADR 0066, 0067).

## The delivery and the wiring

The delivery layer (controllers, inputs, resources, presenters, readers, security) uses only the application's requests and responses; only the ports' adapters use the domain (`tests/Architecture/DomainBehindTheApplicationTest`, ADR 0035). An API context uses nothing from another context but its Contract (the core's `<Context>/Contract`: published queries and integration events, `tests/Architecture/BoundedContextsTest`, ADR 0045, 0047). Security reads who is calling with the `ConsultMyAccount` query (identities and roles, ADR 0040). Repositories answer the domain's questions (`isEmailUsed()`) and decide nothing. The email reservation written with the aggregate (below) is the safety net of `EmailUniqueness` when two requests take the same email at once (ADR 0039, 0048). `tests/Architecture/BoundedContextsTest` fails when a context folder uses anything from another context except its Contract, anticorruption adapters included.

- A context's adapters are grouped by kind: its repositories in `src/<Context>/Persistence`, the anticorruption layers of its ports to other contexts in `src/<Context>/AntiCorruption` (ADR 0086). A console command that uses the adapters of several contexts has its own folder, `src/<Purpose>` (`src/Keys`, `src/Consistency`), never `src/Shared`, which uses no context (`tests/Architecture/BoundedContextsTest`).
- Ports are aliased to adapters in `config/services.yaml`.
- Every core `*Handler` is a service, tagged by its interface. `Shared\DependencyInjection\RefusalTranslatingHandlersPass` decorates it with the decorators of its kind, from the innermost to the outermost (`COMMAND_DECORATORS`, `EVENT_DECORATORS`, `QUERY_DECORATORS`, ADR 0094, 0095):
  - handlers implementing `Prospecter\Core\SharedKernel\Application\CommandHandler` are registered on `command.bus` through `TransactionalCommandHandler`, then `RefusalTranslatingCommandHandler`;
  - handlers implementing `EventHandler` go through `TransactionalEventHandler`, then `RefusalTranslatingEventHandler`, tagged with the id of the handler they decorate as `alias`. (Messenger calls a handler of a given name once per message, parent classes included: decorated the same way, the translator of `ContactsLeftWithoutOrganization` and that of its parent `OrganizationEvent` would be one.) A translator (`Application/Publish…/Publish…Handler`) sits on `event.bus`. `Shared\DependencyInjection\SubscriptionsPass` takes every other one off it; it is reached as the subscription that names it in `config/subscriptions.json` (`Shared\Messaging\Subscriptions`, `contexts.md`). A reaction that no subscription names fails the build. A subscription names one reaction (`handler`) or one per event it listens to (`handlers`), reached by its name and the event's detail-type (`Subscriptions::reactionKey()`, ADR 0104);
  - commands and translators are routed by the type of the message their handler takes;
  - the same pass wraps every `QueryHandler` in a `RefusalTranslatingQueryHandler` and aliases it by its intent's name. A class calling one therefore asks for the core's interface, `QueryHandler $consultOrganization` (`ConsultOrganizationHandler`), never for the decorator (ADR 0035, 0044, 0094);
  - `RebuildFiguresHandler` alone is not a bus service: its steps commit their own units, and `app:reporting:rebuild` calls it (ADR 0103, 0104);
  - the services the contexts offer each other are aliased by their interface to the handler implementing it, and requested by their type (`ConsultMembersService $consultMembersService`, ADR 0089).
- Nothing is built in a constructor, and no service is built by code: everything is injected (`make lint` checks it, ADR 0044). The hexagon's services are registered in `config/services.yaml` (`config/packages/messenger.yaml`: no transport, handled within the request).
- The `Events` port is `Shared\Messaging\RoutingEvents` (ADR 0097). A domain event goes to its context's translators on `event.bus` (synchronous, `allow_no_handlers`). Then, like an integration event, it is encoded (`contexts.md`) and handed to the `Shared\Messaging\Delivery` that `INTEGRATION_EVENTS_DELIVERY` names:
  - `sync`, `SynchronousDelivery`: calls right away the reactions of the subscriptions listening to its source and detail-type, internal ones included, without an inbox, in no promised order (ADR 0097);
  - `outbox`, `OutboxDelivery`: writes it in the commit's outbox, relayed to the bus after the commit, so no reaction runs within the command (ADR 0098, `persistence.md`). This is the delivery of the local stack and of the instances (ADR 0108).
- Reactions take a domain event of their own context or an integration event of another context (ADR 0042, 0046). With the `sync` delivery (the tests, or an instance that names it), they join the unit of work of the command that published: a reaction that is refused or fails makes the command refused or failed, and the command writes nothing (`persistence.md`, ADR 0095). With `outbox`, the worker runs each reaction in its own transaction (`contexts.md`, ADR 0101).
- A gesture runs one use case: the console commands that open an account or register a member dispatch `OpenAccount` or `RegisterAMember` alone, and the member completes their profile in the SPA (ADR 0100). The exception is an AI recording a contact and their organization in one call (`Identification\ContactRecorder`, ADR 0177): everything is read first, then each use case runs in its own unit of work, idempotent step by step. When a later step fails, the organization the call created stops being prospected.
- `bin/console debug:messenger` (`make commands`) lists both buses.

## Checking the consistency

`bin/console app:consistency:check` (`--account=<id>` for one account; `make console CMD="app:consistency:check"`, with `INSTANCE=` on an instance) checks the outcome of the asynchronous reactions rather than watching their failures (ADR 0105). It runs on demand and only reads:

- **the figures**: each account's five figures (`activeOrganizations`, `locatedOrganizations`, `contacts`, `exchanges`, and each month's exchanges that took place, done or missed), counted again from the Prospecting and PersonalData tables (a `Query` counting on `GSI1`; the items read for the organizations placed on the map and for the exchanges, with their status and month), against Reporting's active version;
- **the reactions between the contexts**: each account has members; each member has their profile in PersonalData; each collected contact is attached to organizations of its account, and no forgotten contact still is; no exchange is kept for an organization no longer prospected, or for none; no tags are kept for an organization no longer prospected or for a forgotten contact (ADR 0138); a frozen campaign keeps no recipient for a forgotten contact, while a started campaign keeps it as erased (ADR 0144);
- **the tags**: the facts Campaigns keeps about each prospected organization (the contacts it names, where it is, ADR 0142) and about each contact (how it is reached, read from its contact details and from the LinkedIn profile among its links) agree with the Prospecting and PersonalData tables, and none is kept for an organization no longer prospected or for a forgotten contact (ADR 0141);
- **the lists**: each account has the key that lists it among the accounts (`GSI1`, `ACCOUNTS`), and each exchange the key that lists it in its account (`GSI2`); `app:reporting:rebuild` counts them through these keys. An item written before these keys existed lacks them, and nothing counts it until `app:keys:rebuild` writes them. The check finds the accounts by their items (a `Scan` of `ACCOUNT#`), never through that list, so that it also checks an account missing from it; it finds the exchanges lacking their key by a `Scan` of `EXCHANGE#`. The figures and the reactions still read an account's exchanges through `GSI2`: when an item is missing from its list, run `app:keys:rebuild` first, then `app:reporting:rebuild`, then check again;
- **the calendars**: each exchange that is still planned and upcoming, held by a member connected to a provider, is synced to their calendar there (its `synced` entry in the Connectors table, ADR 0133);
- **the links**: no two contacts of an account record the same link, as their own or as a former one. Two contacts recorded before links were unique are shown by their ids, never by the link, so that the members merge them or correct one of them by hand (`Consistency\ReferencesOfTheContacts`, ADR 0159, ADR 0165);
- **the failure queues**: the messages waiting, visible or not, in the worker's queue (`INTEGRATION_FAILURES_QUEUE`) and the relay's (`RELAY_FAILURES_QUEUE`), whatever the account.

It lists each discrepancy with its account, what it concerns (a figure, an identity, a queue) and the command that corrects it, and it fails when there is one:

- a figure: `app:reporting:rebuild`;
- a Campaigns fact missing or out of date: `app:tags:rebuild --account=<id>`, a fact left behind being a missed reaction;
- an item missing from its list: `app:keys:rebuild`;
- a missed reaction: `app:integration:redrive` when the worker's failures keep its event, otherwise `app:outbox:republish --since=<moment of the gesture>`;
- a relay failure: `app:outbox:republish`;
- an exchange missing from its calendar: its holder pushes it again once it failed, reconnects, or changes it;
- two contacts recording the same link: the members merge them or correct one of them.

It corrects nothing. It counts with direct requests on the tables, never through the services the contexts publish or their retrievers, which would hide their own bugs: `Consistency\ContextTables` is the only code that reads the tables of several contexts, and it only reads them (`tests/Architecture/TablePerContextTest`).

## Hosts

- `APP_API_HOST` (`api.prospecter.localhost`) serves the routes in `src/{Me,Prospecting,PersonalData,SignIn,Dashboard,Contacts,Exchanges,ToDos,Connectors,Tags,Campaigning,Booking}/Controller` (`config/routes.yaml`) and `src/Doc` (`/favicon.ico`, `config/routes/nelmio_api_doc.yaml`; `VersionedNelmioAssets` requests the Swagger UI files from the CDN at the installed NelmioApiDocBundle version). Locally, nginx passes `/cognito/` on the same host to MiniStack's Cognito, for the SPA.
- Outside `/api/` and its contract (ADR 0156, 0157, `mcp.md`):
  - the MCP server on `/mcp` (firewall `mcp`, `config/packages/mcp.yaml`, the route of type `mcp`). Its tools, `src/<Context>/Mcp/<Intent>Tool.php`, one per intent, call the core the way a controller does (`src/Me/Mcp/WhoAmITool`);
  - the protected resource metadata, `/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource/mcp` (`SignIn\Controller\ShowProtectedResourceController`, public);
  - to come, the facade that signs the AIs in on Cognito, `src/SignIn/OAuth` (`/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server`, `/oauth/authorize`, `/oauth/callback`, `/oauth/token`). There the protocol imposes two exceptions to the one-intent-per-endpoint rule: `/oauth/token` carries two intents through its `grant_type`, and `/oauth/authorize` and `/oauth/callback` only redirect.
- Files are not the API's business: the storage app (`apps/storage`, `static.prospecter.localhost`) keeps and serves them, and `Shared\ImageResourceFactory` names their sizes by its convention (`<public url>/<size>/<path>`, ADR 0033).
