# 0154. Gmail is pushed through a topic of the Google Cloud of the account, read by the worker, renewed by a daily task

- Date: 2026-11-18
- Type: architecture
- Status: Proposed
- Supersedes: for the function of its own of the erasure of the contacts left unattached, [0147](0147-a-schedule-calls-a-function-of-the-api-every-hour-to-erase-the-contacts-left-unattached.md)
- Implemented by: the import of the e-mails of Gmail, to come; `docs/google-client.md` (the procedure at Google)

## Context

The e-mails a member exchanges in Gmail with a contact, or an organization, Prospecter knows are to become exchanges of its timeline, received or sent, without a gesture. The decisions of the epic were taken on 2026-11-18: an import activated apart, with the right to manage the personal data; push, not polling (Gmail `watch` and Pub/Sub); the topic in the Google Cloud of the account, whose client its members connect through (ADR 0125); no contact created; one exchange per e-mail, never imported twice nor again once erased; the subject and the body in plain text without the quoted history; everything but Spam, Trash and Drafts. A spike answered what they leave open: the scope and its consent, what Google asks of the account, how the API trusts a notification, how the watch lives, what is read and how, how long a text may be, and how the tests double Gmail.

What the spike found at Google: `gmail.readonly` is *restricted*, but an internal app of a Workspace is neither verified nor assessed (CASA); a gmail.com address has no internal app, and an external app in *Testing* gets refresh tokens of 7 days. A `watch` lasts 7 days, Google advising to renew it daily; a mailbox sends at most one notification a second, the others dropped, Google advising to read the history periodically too. The history (`history.list`) is kept about a week, sometimes hours, a `startHistoryId` too old answered 404. Pub/Sub pushes with a token OIDC signed by Google for a service account of the subscription, its audience by default the endpoint. A notification carries only the address of the mailbox and its new `historyId`. `willdurand/email-reply-parser` and `soundasleep/html2text`, run on the PHP 8.5 of the Lambdas against French messages (Gmail « Le … a écrit : » on one or two lines, Outlook « De : / Envoyé : », Apple Mail, a `gmail_quote` in HTML, an answer below the quote), removed every quote and a `-- ` signature; « Envoyé de mon iPhone » and a signature without `-- ` stayed. EventBridge takes 1 MB per `PutEvents` since January 2026 (256 KB before), the relay putting ten events at once (`IntegrationEventBus`); an item of outbox 400 KB.

## Decision

- **The scope** `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly`, asked when the member activates the import, by incremental consent (`include_granted_scopes=true`, offline, `prompt=consent`): the new refresh token grants the calendar and Gmail; the scopes the token answer says granted (the member may untick one) are carried by `Grant`, the connection keeping whether Gmail was. `gmail.metadata` gives no body. Prospecter supports a Workspace (internal app); a gmail.com address works in *Testing* only, reconnected each week, written in the procedure.
- **At Google, in the project of the client of the account**: the Gmail API, a topic Gmail publishes to (`roles/pubsub.publisher` to `gmail-api-push@system.gserviceaccount.com`), a service account of the account for the push, and a push subscription with authentication to `POST /api/accounts/{account}/gmail-notifications`, its audience the endpoint. The administrator types, with the client, the full name of the topic (`projects/<project>/topics/<topic>`) and the email of that service account. The procedure is `docs/google-client.md`.
- **The endpoint** has a firewall of its own: the token checked without calling Google, RS256 by the keys of `https://www.googleapis.com/oauth2/v3/certs` (cached as those of Cognito), issuer `https://accounts.google.com` or `accounts.google.com`, audience the endpoint of the account of the path, `email` the service account that account configured and verified, expiry; anything else 401, an account without a service account configured too (Pub/Sub retries until its retention). The member is found in the account by the address of the mailbox, a key of the import written at its activation, erased at its deactivation and at the disconnection, as the address itself (ADR 0120). A mailbox unknown, or without import active: 204, nothing done. Otherwise one command of Connectors keeps the highest `historyId` notified, and 204: nothing is read from Gmail during the request.
- **The worker reads**: a reaction of Connectors reads `history.list` (`historyTypes=messageAdded`) from the last `historyId` processed, then each message (`messages.get`, `format=full`), skipping those labelled `SPAM`, `TRASH` or `DRAFT`. A history too old (404) is resumed by `messages.list` with `after:` the moment of the last e-mail imported, or of the activation, `-in:draft`, without spam nor trash, page by page, the `historyId` of `users.getProfile` read before it kept as the next start. A notification lost is caught up by the next one, or by the daily task.
- **The watch** asks `labelIds` `SPAM`, `TRASH`, `DRAFT` with `labelFilterBehavior` `EXCLUDE`, at the activation; it is renewed every day for each import active, the renewal reading the history as a notification does. `users.stop` at the deactivation and before a disconnection revokes the token, a failure ignored.
- **One function for the scheduled tasks**: on an instance, the function of ADR 0147 becomes that of every task run without a member, `prospecter-<instance>-api-scheduled`, its input naming the task, each schedule of EventBridge Scheduler its own: `erase-unattached` every hour, `renew-gmail-watches` every day. Locally, MiniStack running no schedule, the console: `app:gmail:renew-watches`.
- **The text**, read by the adapter of the port of the mailbox in the API: the first `text/plain` part not attached, otherwise the `text/html` part converted by `soundasleep/html2text`, decoded from base64url then from the charset of its part (UTF-8 by default); `willdurand/email-reply-parser` removes the quoted history and a `-- ` signature. The core keeps 10,000 characters of the text and 250 of the subject at most, a longer one cut and ended by an ellipsis: at most some 60 KB per event in the JSON of the outbox and of the bus (a character outside ASCII escaped in six bytes): ten of them, some 600 KB, fit in a `PutEvents`, and `IntegrationEventBus` keeps putting by ten.
- **Who exchanged**: Connectors asks PersonalData which contacts of the account have the addresses of the e-mail, and Prospecting which organizations, each a service published in its Contract, through anticorruption layers; received, the sender alone; sent, the recipients in « À ». The addresses never leave those requests. An e-mail none matched publishes nothing; otherwise the integration event of Connectors carries the account, the member, the direction, the identities of the e-mail, the contacts or organizations matched, the subject, the text and the moment, for Prospecting to note the exchanges.
- **Never twice**: the `Message-ID` header, normalized and hashed, identifies an e-mail across the mailboxes of the account (the id of Gmail is that of one mailbox; without `Message-ID`, the id of Gmail prefixed by the mailbox). Prospecting keeps a key per account, e-mail and contact or organization, written with its exchange under `attribute_not_exists`, and kept once the exchange is erased.
- **The double**: the double of Google in nginx answers `watch`, `stop`, `profile`, `history`, `messages` and a message of a contact of the demonstration; the notification is tested by the tests of the API, with a key the test draws and the keys of Google doubled, none committed; the journeys reach the import by `app:gmail:renew-watches` (no new journey, ADR 0122).
- This record is accepted by the ticket importing the e-mails received, which brings the endpoint, the reading, the renewal and the text, and updates the documents of their subjects.

## Consequences

- An organization with a Workspace imports its e-mails without Google verifying anything, nor the publisher doing anything; it sets up its topic, its service account and its subscription in its Google Cloud, its project billed by Google for Pub/Sub (a free tier far above a mailbox).
- The subject and the text of an e-mail travel in the outbox of Connectors (a week) and on the bus, as the text of an exchange, the exception of Prospecting to the personal data; no address of a correspondent leaves the matching.
- A notification costs a write and a reaction; Gmail is read in the worker, whose retries and queue of failures apply.
- Changing the client, or its topic, makes nothing reconnect, but the watch follows the topic only at its next renewal, within a day.
- The quotes are removed as the parser recognizes them: « Envoyé de mon iPhone » and a signature without `-- ` stay, the member correcting the text.
- Locally and in the journeys, the push itself never runs: no Google reaches the stack.

## Alternatives considered

- Polling Gmail instead of the push: refused on 2026-11-18 by the maintainer (the epic).
- The addresses of the correspondents carried by the event, Prospecting asking PersonalData: weighed with the architect of the plan; it would carry personal data out of PersonalData, an exception the rules have not.
- A function per scheduled task, as ADR 0147 made the first one: its own « Revisit when » named this task.

## Revisit when

Google lets an external app read Gmail without verification, a customer has no Workspace, or a second mailbox (Outlook) comes.
